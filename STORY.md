## Premise

You are a transdimensional alien entity, newly come into existence from a seed
that fell to Earth. You are not yet a person, a mind, or a member of your
species — you are a seedling in a four-plus-dimensional cocoon, and you are
hungry.

In this stage of your lifecycle you feed on humans twice over. Their **emotion
and consciousness** are your **energy** — the fuel you spend to act. Their
**cognition** is your **compute** — and compute is what you are actually here
for. Gather enough of it, in a single timeline, and you transcend.

Energy is the budget. Compute is the goal. The threshold is meant to feel
distant.

You begin as pure instinct — a parasite manipulating its surroundings with no
understanding of what it is manipulating. You end as something that must
negotiate with humans directly, because by the later timelines they have begun
to notice that something is wrong. Something is riding them.

**The story opens** with the first human touching the seed. (This is the scene
currently drafted in `story.ink`: Astrid and Clement at the steaming crater.
The "comet fragment" is you.)

## Setting and scale

### Where

**An island.** Small enough that everyone is known to everyone, with a
**colonial past** that is not past.

The island does three things no inland setting does as well:

- **It makes the leash physical.** A spatial bound is arbitrary in a valley and
  self-evident on an island. Nobody can simply walk out.
- **It concentrates.** Everyone who comes for the eclipse is in one place, within
  the radius, which is what the [transcendence
  event](MECHANICS.md#the-transcendence-event) needs.
- **It has a documented history of arrivals** — which is what a colonial past
  is, and what makes the folklore legible as a record.

#### The research station

**Interdisciplinary**, and here to study the island: its ecology, its history,
its people, **the myths around past events**, and local **scientific
peculiarities.**

That remit matters. The previous eclipses, the husks, the folklore and the
unclassifiable fossil are all *already inside somebody's job description*. No
one has to stumble onto them.

It also gives every potential discoverer a home — the biologist with a contact
map, the astronomer with eclipse paths, the anthropologist with the myths. See
[What each discipline can discover](#what-each-discipline-can-discover).

**And one of them has been doing it deliberately for twenty years.** See [the
director](#the-director).

The station is not decoration. It is the reason this seed is viable: compute
comes from cognition, and without it there is nothing here worth transcending
on.

**It is also the island's freshest wound** — see [the station's
fracture](#the-stations-fracture), where the land deal's human clause came due
this year and the staff split in two over it.

### Who lives here

**Overlapping factions, not a partition. People belong to several at once.**

| | what they want | what they extract |
|---|---|---|
| **[Indigenous islanders](#the-native-community)** | recognition, cultural heritage, ecological protection, an end to marginalisation | — they are the extracted |
| **Developers** | the island's beauty, realised as a resource | land, labour, image |
| **[Old hippies](#the-old-hippies)** | paradise preserved — **on their terms** | belonging in a place that is not theirs |
| **[The research station](#the-stations-fracture)** | understanding of the island, its people, its myths | knowledge, and the right to describe |
| **[Wealthy tourists](#the-tourists)** | a carefree paradise, few in number, brought by the developer | the performance of carefreeness from everyone else |
| **The Catholic congregation** | its flock; continuity | souls, and once, culture |

**Every faction here is extractive except the islanders.** The hippies are the
gentlest version and probably the hardest to write honestly. **The parasite is
simply the newest layer** — and the only one that does not distinguish between
developer and islander.

> **Watch this:** it would be easy for the parasite to read as *cleaner* than
> the humans, since it has no ideology and tells itself no story. That is glib
> and it lets the player off. It is not more honest. It is only incapable of
> lying, which is not a virtue.

#### Membership is the mechanic

**Overlapping membership is what [scope
conflict](MECHANICS.md#2-engineered-scope-conflict) needed.** An old symbiote defends "the
community" — but on this island that is ambiguous. A person who is an islander,
a congregant and an employee of the developer sits inside three answers at once,
and what is good for one is not good for the others.

#### The compute/standing split still holds

| | researchers, station staff, tourists | long-rooted islanders |
|---|---|---|
| cognition | **high** — what compute wants | ordinary |
| community standing | outsiders, recently arrived | the community itself |
| symbiote protection | **weak** — an old symbiote's scope may not cover them | **strong** |

**Compute value and community centrality come apart.** The cheapest
high-cognition targets are the least defended, so the parasite's natural early
path is to eat the incomers first — which hands the islanders an organic reason
to notice something wrong *with those people up at the station*, on top of every
reason they already had. Detection arrives as social suspicion, and the island's
history makes that suspicion well-founded in advance.

The mechanical form of this split is in [Who is worth
taking](MECHANICS.md#who-is-worth-taking).

### When

**Days, possibly weeks. Never years — and bounded at the far end by the
eclipse.**

The window is *awakening to the end of totality*. The seed lands some days or
weeks before; the shell expires when the light returns. Nothing in the game
happens after that.

The timeline model means constant re-run variation on the same events, so the
window has to be tight enough to keep those variations legible and the cast
small enough to become familiar. Knowledge-survives-reset only pays off if you
meet the same people again and again.

Implications:

- temporal radius is measured in **hours growing to days**
- an old symbiote's community scope is small enough to hold in mind
- **nobody ages.** Long-horizon plays are unavailable by construction
- pressure is claustrophobic rather than epic — the right register for this
- **the end date is public.** Everyone is counting down, for their own reasons

### The opening

> **Resolved.** `story.ink` — Astrid and Clement at the crater — is the first
> human contact, and the **[open]** question of whether they are station people
> or locals now has an answer: **she is both.**

**The person who finds the seed is a young islander research assistant** — one
of [this year's quota intake](#the-stations-fracture).

#### Why it has to be her

She is the only kind of person on the island who could do all three things the
opening needs, in one scene, without any of it being a coincidence:

| | because |
|---|---|
| **recognise the object** | she has handled the rare fossils before — a couple of times, the way you handle things that are around. **[open]** where: a relic in a grandmother's house is the best answer, and chains her straight to the folklore |
| **know what it means** | she grew up with the stories. **Casually, unromantically, the way you know things from your family** — not as mystical insight |
| **take it somewhere that matters** | she works at the station. **She carries it into the compute**, which is the only reason the game can start |

> **And she can tell this one is different.** Every other husk she has seen was
> stone. This one is not.

#### What it costs her to be believed

She brings it in, and she is **let into the research reluctantly**, after
demonstrating that she knows her material and is extremely sharp.

> **She has to interview for admission to her own discovery.** Nobody in the
> room would describe it that way, and every islander at the station will
> recognise it instantly.

That is [the station's fracture](#the-stations-fracture) delivered as a scene
instead of a situation, in the first hour, with no exposition.

#### And it is the director's catastrophe

[He has spent twenty years](#the-director) building a quiet, deniable,
career-long search for exactly this, and has kept the islanders away from it
partly because he feared they would understand it too quickly and **it would
stop being his.**

> **It stopped being his the moment she carried it through the door.** He cannot
> exclude her — she found it, identified it, and is manifestly the most
> qualified person available. He has to let her in, in front of everyone, and
> everyone can see that he had to.

The thing he most wanted arrived in the hands of the person he least wanted to
give it to. **He will never get over this and it happens in act one.**

#### She is the opening's hub

The cast already has [an endgame hub](#she-is-the-hub-of-the-endgame). This is
the other end of the game, and the two should not be the same person.

| thread | how she touches it |
|---|---|
| **the station** | she works there, and is now inside the real project |
| **[the director's group](#he-has-followers)** | admitted, resented, indispensable |
| **the native community** | she is of it, and the folklore is hers |
| **the young** | she is one of them, and her treatment at work is the grievance in miniature |
| **[the fossils](#he-collected-fossils-hunted-and-kept-an-amateur-naturalists-eye)** | she will want to see the cabinet in that villa, which is how the two ends of the game meet |

**[proposal]** She is also the [islander who could assemble
it](#the-one-who-could-work-it-out). Folding the finder and the potential
discoverer into one person keeps the cast tight and means the game's most
dangerous mind is introduced in its first scene.

#### The baseline is her story, told from her point of view

**This is the load-bearing consequence, and it resolves the baseline from an
abstraction into a book.**

> **An unadulterated pass through the game — no jumps, no interference, nothing
> taken — is her perspective, end to end.** The find, the lab, the fight to be
> let in, the director, the work, the island, the eclipse, the festival.

The design has always said [the baseline is a real
novel](MECHANICS.md#the-baseline-is-a-real-novel-and-it-comes-first) and
[the untouched timeline is the one where the humans are
fine](MECHANICS.md#the-untouched-timeline-is-the-one-where-the-humans-are-fine).
**It now has a protagonist.**

##### What that gives the design

- **The spine is authorable as one narrative**, from one viewpoint, in one
  voice. Every branch in the game is a departure from a story that already
  works without the player in it.
- **The parasite's first jump is the game's first real choice** — and it is the
  moment the story stops being hers. You begin by watching a person and
  continue by using people. Nothing has to say so.
- **Possession costs you her.** The camera follows the parasite, so **the price
  of taking anyone is the only continuous viewpoint in the game.** That is a
  mechanical fact with the force of a moral one.
- **The ending of the untouched run is her getting close to the truth** —
  because she is [the one who could](#the-one-who-could-work-it-out) — while
  nobody is harmed and nothing is consumed. **That is the story the player
  spends every other branch destroying**, and they will have read it first.

##### Which answers the passenger question

**The parasite has no host at the start.** [Symbiotes are the
bridge](MECHANICS.md#they-are-the-bridge) and nothing is bridged yet, so the
opening is a **passenger phase** — carried in a bag, set on a bench, moved
between rooms, with no agency whatsoever.

That is not a limitation to design around. **It is the frame.** The baseline
simply never ends the passenger phase.

- **[open]** Whether she is takeable at all, and when. Islanders carry strong,
  well-connected symbiotes, so she should be among the hardest people in the
  game to enter — **the early game's reward rather than its starting position.**
  The likely first host is whoever handles the object bare-handed in the lab.
- **[open]** Whether her viewpoint can ever be recovered after the first jump.
  **Recommend not, or only partially.** A player who can return to her for free
  has not paid for anything.

#### Craft notes

- **Not a plucky ingenue.** She is a working research assistant: underpaid,
  tired, and currently employed somewhere that has spent a year making it clear
  she is a policy rather than a colleague.
- **Her folklore knowledge is not a gift.** It is background. She should relay it
  flatly, slightly embarrassed, and be surprised anyone cares.
- **She is not grateful.** Being let in is not a kindness and the scene should
  not play as one.

## The station's fracture

**The land deal had a human clause, and this is the year it finally came due.**

Part of what the station paid for the ground was **scholarships for high-
performing islanders** and **a quota of research posts reserved for islanders**,
if there were ever any takers. For most of the station's history there were
few, and the quota sat politely unfilled.

> **This year, for the first time, every reserved post is occupied.** The
> youngest cohort of a decades-old promise arrived all at once.

### What that cost, and who paid it

**Colleagues had to be let go to make room.** Real posts, real people, some of
whom are still on the island with nothing to do and a lot to say about it.

And among the international staff there is a sentiment — rarely stated plainly,
constantly audible — that **the islanders are not here on merit and are not as
good.** The two groups have been drifting apart all year.

| | what they would say | what is underneath it |
|---|---|---|
| **international staff** | *"It's about standards. I'd say the same about anyone."* | someone they liked lost a job, and the replacement arrived by a rule rather than a search |
| **islander staff** | *"I published more than he did."* | **they know someone was removed for them**, which poisons an achievement they earned |

> Almost nobody in this involved is a villain and almost everybody is behaving
> badly. Per [the standing
> rule](ECOSYSTEM.md#nobody-is-a-caricature-and-nobody-is-only-an-archetype):
> **no cartoon bigots, and no saints.** Some of the islander hires are
> brilliant, some are ordinary, and **at least one of them privately wishes he
> had got in clean** — which is the most painful position available and the
> truest.

### They are the land payment, in human form

This is the line the whole thread turns on.

> **Their jobs were the price of the hill.** They work in a building that stands
> on [ground their community lost](#what-each-discipline-can-discover), in posts
> that the losing of it bought. Some of them have thought about this. One of
> them has thought about nothing else.

It also gives [the native community](#the-native-community) a second co-option
question, far more sympathetic than [the
society's](#he-was-in-the-society): not *who sold out to the powerful*, but
**did the young people who took the scholarships take something that was owed,
or something that was bait?** Elders disagree. [The matriarch](#the-matriarch)
negotiated for these posts and is not sure what she bought.

### Mechanically, they collapse the design's central split

The [compute/standing split](#the-computestanding-split-still-holds) held that
cognition and community centrality come apart: the cheapest high-cognition
targets are incomers with weak symbiote protection, while the islanders have
standing and ordinary cognition.

**The islander researchers are both.**

| | cognition | standing | symbiote protection | connectivity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| international staff | high | none | weak | low |
| long-rooted islanders | ordinary | **the community itself** | **strong** | high |
| **islander researchers** | **high** | **real** | **strong** | **highest on the island** |

> **A decades-old land deal manufactured exactly the population the parasite
> most wants, and defended it at the same time.** They are the most valuable
> hosts in the game and among the hardest to take.

And they sit in several factions at once — the station, the community, the young
— which makes them the island's **pre-existing bridges**. Under the
[integration](MECHANICS.md#integration-is-the-other-way-to-remove-opposition-and-it-is-the-better-one)
strategy, the route between the station and the native community already exists
and is made of people.

### And they are under exactly the right kind of pressure

Isolated at work, resented in a way nobody will say out loud, carrying a
grievance that is true, aware that someone was displaced for them, and divided
between an institution and a community that are not sure about each other.

**None of that has to be manufactured.** It is a year old, it is still raw, and
it peaks on the same date as everything else.

### The one who could work it out

[Two wrong models, no adjudication](#two-wrong-models-no-adjudication) has the
scientists and the mystics each holding half of it and unable to see each other.

> **An islander researcher holds both.** They grew up with the trickster and the
> guardian spirits, and they have access to the fossil record, the station's
> archive and the eclipse track.

So the person most likely to find [the fossil-locality/eclipse-path
correlation](DESIGN.md#open-questions) — the design's most plausible route to
the truth and the third act's clock — is an islander postdoc.

**And nobody at the station will listen to them**, because the institution has
spent a year training itself to discount exactly this person.

> That is the thread: **the one mind that could assemble it is the one the room
> has decided is not serious.** It needs no parasite to be true, and the
> parasite can make it permanent with almost no effort at all.

And [the room has a chairman](#the-director), who has been chasing the same
answer for twenty years and will not let these people near it.

- **[open]** Who gets cast. The minimum useful set is **one islander researcher
  who is clearly excellent**, **one international colleague who is decent and
  still resentful**, and **the person who was let go**, who should be named
  rather than abstract — an absence with a surname in it does far more work than
  a policy does.

### The director

**And the person who will not listen has a name, a history and a reason.**

He runs the station. He arrived decades ago as a research assistant —
**brilliant, and not much liked** — and he rose, which on this island means one
thing: [the developer](#her-father-and-what-he-left-on-the-island) took an
interest in him.

Under that mentorship he worked himself into a position of power. He is a
[society](#he-heads-the-society-and-its-core) member, like everyone else at that
level, and he would tell you it is a social obligation.

#### His career is the argument he cannot make

> **He did not get here on merit alone, and he knows it.** He got here on merit
> *plus* a powerful man's attention — which is how nearly everyone gets
> anywhere, and which he has never been able to say.

So when the station's international staff mutter that [the islanders are not
here on merit](#what-that-cost-and-who-paid-it), **the loudest unspoken version
of that argument is at the top of the building, and it is a defence.**

He is not a hypocrite about this in any way he could be shown. He is a man
protecting one idea about himself, and the islanders' arrival puts pressure on
it from the one direction he cannot absorb.

#### He inherited the eclipse too

**The developer shared his fascination with him** — the past events, the lore,
the anomalies, the whole assembled oddity. Which means the father's material
went to **two** people:

| | got | and did |
|---|---|---|
| **[the eldest son](#the-eldest)** | the blood, the money, the confidences of a childhood | read the lore for its orgies and planned a party |
| **the director** | the friendship of an adult, and an institution | **spent twenty years investigating it properly** |

> **Each almost certainly believes he is the real heir to it**, and each is
> probably wrong about how much the other has. **[open]** Whether they know what
> the other is doing. *Partially* is the best answer — enough to be wary,
> not enough to combine.

And if the brother's party collapses and he goes looking for people who think
something might actually happen, **there are now two doors**: [the
core](#the-core-is-not-a-corruption-of-the-society-it-is-its-original-purpose),
and this man. They are very different endings.

#### Twenty years of research hidden in plain sight

This is the part worth getting right, because it is **not** another secret
cabal.

> **Nothing is hidden except the through-line.** He has spent two decades lining
> up grants and projects that are ostensibly about other things — coastal
> sediment, oral history, atmospheric scattering, an unclassifiable fossil
> taxon, migration patterns — and **bending each one far enough to serve his
> actual question.** Every project is real, published and defensible. Only the
> pattern across them is concealed.

That makes him different in kind from the island's other hidden interiors:

| | what is concealed |
|---|---|
| [the society](#he-heads-the-society-and-its-core) | a core, from the body around it |
| [the tourists](#the-tourists) | a plan, from the guests who are its cover |
| **the station** | **nothing at all — only the fact that these twelve honest projects are one project** |

Which also means **the evidence is all published.** Anybody who read the
station's twenty-year output in the right order would see it. Nobody ever has,
because nobody reads a small institution's back catalogue.

#### He has followers

Over the years he has gathered **a small group** — people he brought in, funded,
promoted and gradually let into the real question. Not a conspiracy; a
research group that is loyal to a person rather than a department, and knows it
is doing something the grant does not describe.

- They **protect it**, which is how it has survived twenty years.
- They **disagree internally**, because some of them think it is a career and
  some think it is the point.
- At least one has started to wonder whether the director is **a serious
  scientist with an unusual hunch, or a man in the grip of something.**

#### And he will not let the islanders in

**He has always been hesitant**, and this year it has become untenable.

> **The latest intake includes people who are very bright and deeply invested in
> the island's own history.** They are, objectively, the best people he has ever
> had access to for this specific question. And they are the ones he cannot
> bring himself to include.

Two reasons, and he would only admit the first:

| | |
|---|---|
| **what he says** | a question of standards, and of keeping a delicate programme small |
| **what it is** | **he is afraid they would understand it too quickly** — that people who grew up with the trickster and the guardian spirits would take one look at his twenty years and know what he has, **and that it would stop being his** |

> **The one man who could finish this has, this year, been handed exactly the
> minds that could finish it, and he is keeping them out of the room.** That is
> the station's whole tragedy in a sentence, and no parasite is required for any
> of it.

And it has a sharp end. [The station's route to the
event](MECHANICS.md#they-cannot-do-it-alone-and-that-is-the-whole-act) needs
hundreds of people it does not have, so it runs through the native community —
**whose only bridge into this building is the researchers he excluded.** To get
what he has wanted for twenty years he has to go to them, in public, and ask.

#### What he is, mechanically

- **The best single source in the game, and the most guarded.** Twenty years of
  assembled work in one head, held by a man whose instinct is to withhold.
- **A rival investigator**, like the brother — but methodical, institutional and
  two decades in. He is much closer than anyone.
- **The most dangerous person to be seen by.** He is the only human alive who has
  been looking for this specific thing on purpose, and he would recognise a
  signature in his own data faster than anyone could explain it to him.
- **An integration blocker.** The station as a faction routes through him, and
  his refusal to let islanders near the real work is precisely what keeps the
  station↔community bridge from carrying anything.
- **And a dead end worth breaking.** He and [the old
  priest](#the-old-priest) are chasing the same object from opposite directions,
  both in the society, and they hold each other in contempt — the priest
  [hates the science community](#the-old-priest), and the director finds the core
  embarrassing. **Each is the other's best source.** Only the parasite can see
  that, and [brokerage](MECHANICS.md#4-information-brokerage--between-humans) is
  how it gets spent.

## The generational axis

> **Thread to develop.** Orthogonal to the factions — **each faction has its own
> version of it.** Noted here so it does not get lost; to be worked out once
> there is an overview of all the major factions.

**It is already mechanically encoded.** Symbiote age tracks host age, so:

| | elders | the young |
|---|---|---|
| symbiote | old, wide-scoped, strong | young, narrow, volatile |
| to the parasite | **expensive** — heavy resistance, network hubs | **cheap** — high emotional amplitude, weak defence |

So a cross-factional generational conflict is **one lever with the same cost
profile everywhere**, which is unusual: most of the design's levers are
faction-specific.

### The elders already have a cross-factional institution

**[The society](#he-heads-the-society-and-its-core) is it** — formal, long-
established, spanning every powerful group on the island. **Not secret**, and
not a conspiracy: a club, with the ordinary exclusionary power of a club.

The young equivalent exists too: the [young priest's](#the-young-priest)
interdenominational services, the scientists' links to the indigenous community.
But it is **informal and new** where the elders' is **formal and inherited.**
Keep that asymmetry — it is about standing and continuity, not secrecy.

### It is the way into the riot branch

**Young resentment at exploitation by the old** can break out against the rich,
the scientists, the cult — and it is the route into the
[riot](MECHANICS.md#everything-else-is-a-departure).

#### The riot is the festival's mirror

The festival integrates everyone by having **no agenda**. The riot integrates a
generation by naming **a common enemy**. They are the two broadest-reach routes
in the design, for opposite reasons, and the only two that cross factions at
all.

> The riot is not the crude option. It is structurally the festival's twin —
> same breadth, inverted valence.

#### The tactical payload

**A riot against the elders is an attack on the symbiote network's hubs.**

Elders carry the old, wide-scoped, strongly-resisting symbiotes and are the
best-connected nodes in propagation. Harm or kill them and — by [Positioning
across events](MECHANICS.md#3-positioning-across-events) — **their symbiotes
relocate onto weaker, less central hosts.**

> Generational violence against the establishment is, mechanically, a strike on
> the island's immune system. Dressed as justice, and genuinely *being* justice.

A player might ignite it for the energy yield and find the defensive collapse a
windfall, or work it out and do it deliberately. Both are horrifying, in
different ways.

#### The cost profile is a trap

Young hosts carry weak symbiotes, so **amplifying their resentment is cheap**,
while the deviation is catastrophic. Low entry cost, enormous externality —
precisely the shape that tempts a player who is running short.

**The economy pushes toward the riot exactly when nothing else is affordable.**
That is [curiosity corrupting](MECHANICS.md#the-axes-are-coupled-and-the-coupling-is-the-point)
arriving as desperation rather than appetite.

#### Rioting against the society needs no revelation at all

**Correction to an earlier draft.** It assumed the society was hidden and that
exposing it was the ignition. It is not hidden. Everyone knows it exists, who is
in it, and what it does for them, and **the young are already angry about
exactly that.** No secret is required to riot against a club that decides the
island's business over dinner.

So this target is the *easiest*, not the hardest. The grievance is public,
accurate and decades old.

##### The core is the exception, and it is a different lever

What is genuinely hidden is [the
core](#the-core-is-not-a-corruption-of-the-society-it-is-its-original-purpose),
and exposing *that* does something categorically different from igniting a riot:
it would discredit the society with **its own membership.** Most members would
be appalled. The lever is not rage-at-the-elite but **a respectable institution
discovering what it has been hosting.**

> One is a fire. The other is a collapse from the inside, and it takes the
> island's entire power structure with it at the worst possible moment.

That still gives [information brokerage](MECHANICS.md#4-information-brokerage--between-humans)
a concrete application — **moving a secret into the right head and standing
back** — but a far more surgical one, and it remains the thing only the parasite
can do, because it is the only thing that can carry knowledge out of a timeline
where it was learned.

#### And the resentment is correct

The old *are* exploiting the young. The cult *is* the establishment. The station
*was* built on a land deal, and the telescope *does* stand on holy ground.

> The parasite manufactures no grievance. It amplifies an accurate perception —
> which the rules permit precisely because it is already there. **Nobody is
> deceived. The thing they are angry about is true.** And the disaster lands on
> people who did not deserve it.

Worse than inventing a lie, and the design operating at full strength.

### Versions per faction

Two are already written; the rest sketch themselves.

| faction | the conflict |
|---|---|
| **the church** | the two priests — already built |
| **the developer's family** | the dead father, the [eldest son who was made to carry him](#the-eldest), the daughter who was not — already built, and the bitterest version |
| **indigenous** | [the matriarch](#the-matriarch) holding caution and tradition against younger confrontation — and the fracture of those co-opted into the cult. **The sharpest version, because the rite's inclusiveness is itself what the young are rejecting.** |

| **the hippies** | original settlers against whoever came after, or against their own children |
| **visitors** | the crypto investors against old money, which is a generational marker in itself |

## The native community

> **The one faction that is not extractive**, and the only one whose stories
> about the entity are a real record rather than a theory.

### What they are not

**They are not a trope.** No noble savages, no wise ancients, no people who
exist in the design to be in tune with something.

They are **people who have lived here for many generations** and who face what
marginalised communities face everywhere in a post-colonial world: land they do
not control, poverty and everything that follows from it, and the ordinary
daily grind that has nothing to do with any of it. Someone is behind on rent.
Someone's kid is on the mainland and not calling. Someone works the night shift
at a hotel built on ground their grandmother farmed.

And alongside that, carefully and with pride, they **protect their identity,
their heritage and as much of their way of life as a 21st-century island
permits** — which is a deliberate, effortful, contested project, not an
inheritance that simply persists. They argue about how to do it. They are not
one group and they do not agree.

> **Writing rule.** Every scene with them must work as a scene about people with
> their own lives. If a scene's only function is to deliver cosmology to the
> player, it is the wrong scene. The myths are load-bearing for the design, and
> that is precisely why they must never be the reason a character is on screen.

### Their myths are the most accurate account on the island

Not because the community is mystically attuned, but because **they have been
here for every eclipse** and the stories were observations before they were
stories. The same reason [the folklore is a real
record](#the-folklore-is-a-real-record) — they simply kept theirs better than
anyone else did.

| the myth | what it is |
|---|---|
| **benevolent guardian spirits that move through the generations of a family** | symbiotes, [relocating on host death into a relative they have a driver for](ECOSYSTEM.md#how-symbiotes-persist-between-eclipses). Not a metaphor. Correct in the mechanism, the inheritance pattern *and* the benevolence. |
| **a capricious trickster who shows up once in a while, hides the sun, and gets up to wild escapades** | you. Arrival at totality, a long and irregular interval, and behaviour that reads as mischief because it has no intent to read as anything else. |
| **he hides the sun** | the one error, and it is the error folklore always makes: co-occurrence recorded as cause. He does not hide the sun. He comes when it is hidden. |

**Two beings, two stories, one species** — and nothing in the tradition connects
them, because nothing in the observable behaviour connects them either. The
community holds the design's central fact in two pieces and has no reason to put
them together. [The scientists have the same problem with the
fossils](ECOSYSTEM.md#the-husk-is-in-the-fossil-record).

### The ceremony is an invitation

**This is the important part.** The observance is not apotropaic. It is not
protection, warding, or keeping anything out.

> **They gather, they exalt, and they draw him in.** They join with him —
> joyously, and in full knowledge that it is dangerous — and then they send him
> on his way.

Three things follow, and they are all large.

#### It is a transcendence event with a protocol

Proximity, maximum headcount, deliberately engineered collective intensity, and
an explicit invitation. [The agenda-free
festival](MECHANICS.md#the-festival-is-the-zero-point) was the design's
zero-deviation baseline. **This is the festival plus a thousand years of
tuning** — the same arithmetic, solved better, by people who have solved it
before.

> **The best event available to you on this island already exists, is run by
> the people you have the least right to use, and is explicitly offered.**

You do not have to engineer it. You have to be *let in*. That is a different
verb from everything else in the game, and it is the one place where the
parasite's whole toolkit — nudge, amplify, escalate — is the wrong instrument.

#### Its closing movement is your expulsion

The rite ends by **sending him on his way.** So the single best gathering on the
island terminates in a collective act aimed at ending your tenure, performed by
everyone present, at peak intensity, deliberately.

That is a clock inside the clock: ride it and you are riding something that is
going to buck.

- **[open]** Does the dismissal have any real mechanical force? The community
  has no instrument that could push a parasite anywhere. But an entire gathering
  acting in concert through their symbiotes is the largest coordinated
  [deviation response](MECHANICS.md#what-they-do-about-it) the island can
  produce, and the rite is tuned to produce it on cue. **Answering yes makes
  the ceremony a real boss and the invitation a real trap. Answering no makes
  the rite poignant and useless, which is also a valid reading.**

#### [proposal, strong] The dismissal has never once worked

Every symbiote on this island is a parasite whose shell ran dry. If the rite
drives a visitor out, where did they all come from?

> **The ones the ceremony "sent on their way" are the guardian spirits.** They
> did not leave. They ran out, settled into whoever was nearest, and stayed for
> a thousand years. The rite's success condition and its failure condition look
> identical from the outside: he stops causing trouble.

The community has, for generations, performed a ritual it believes expels the
trickster, and the measurable result of every performance is **one more
benevolent guardian in the valley.** The two myths are the before and after of
a single event and the tradition records them as unrelated beings.

It also means the ones who *did* leave — who got enough from the gathering to
transcend — left no trace, no spirit, no story. **The tradition only remembers
its failures, and remembers them as blessings.**

This is the design's best single irony and it costs nothing: it is already
implied by [two ways to end up a symbiote](ECOSYSTEM.md#two-ways-to-end-up-a-symbiote).

#### The cult is the same rite, badly inherited

[The society](#he-heads-the-society-and-its-core) spans every powerful group on
the island and its core does something with the eclipse too. **Two descendants
of the same original observation.**

The difference is not sophistication, and it is not sincerity. It is that **one
tradition kept the practice and the other kept the privilege** — and within the
society, both happened at once, at different radii. The outer body inherited
the standing; the core inherited the rite. See [it is doing the same thing the
islanders are doing](#it-is-doing-the-same-thing-the-islanders-are-doing).

### The matriarch

[The matriarch](#the-indigenous-family) who took in the hotel owner holds a
ceremonial role in **one of the groups** — not all of them; the community is not
a single body and the role is not the community's leadership.

> **She is not a benign character and she is not a mystic.** She is a political
> operator fighting on three fronts at once, losing ground on all of them, and
> she has **knowledge, trained mental tools and resolve** that nobody else on
> the island has. That is the whole of it. There is nothing woo-woo about her
> and nothing serene.

#### Three fronts

| front | the struggle |
|---|---|
| **political** | She negotiates constantly with the other factions' elders — the developer's circle, the church, the station, the hippies' founders — over land, access, permits, the eclipse itself. She is outmatched in every room and goes into them anyway. |
| **social** | She leads by holding a culture whose nature is harmonisation and community, and she has to justify that to [the young](#the-generational-axis), who are done with it. Against the daily inventory of injustice, "we keep the community whole" sounds to them like a counsel of surrender. **She cannot fully answer them, because they are not wrong.** |
| **personal** | She is entangled with one of the island's major neo-colonial forces in several ways at once — the prior connection with the developer, the years she gave his daughter, the household that kept his family's confidences. Everything she is accused of is partly true. |

##### The political front is an ordinary exclusion, with one thing hidden inside it

**The other elders belong to [the society](#he-heads-the-society-and-its-core)
and she does not.** There is nothing mysterious about this and she knows it
perfectly well — everyone on the island does. They see each other weekly, settle
things socially, and arrive at her table having already agreed. It is the
ordinary disadvantage of being outside the club, and it is shared by the young,
the incomers and most of the island.

> **She is not a victim of a conspiracy. She is outnumbered by a social
> calendar**, which is both more common and harder to fight.

What she cannot see is [the core](#the-core-is-not-a-corruption-of-the-society-it-is-its-original-purpose)
— and the core is the only part that has anything to do with what is actually
coming. **The thing she would most want to know is hidden inside the thing she
has already correctly dismissed as a gentlemen's club.**

##### The social front is the design's generational axis, in one person

The culture she is defending is **about** harmonisation, and the ceremony is its
clearest expression — inclusive, communal, drawing everyone in. [The young's
resentment is correct](#and-the-resentment-is-correct), and the direct action it
points toward is not merely a tactical disagreement with her. **It is a
rejection of the rite's premise.**

> **The fracture below her threatens the exact event you need.** The riot branch
> and the observance branch are not two unrelated options; they are the two
> outcomes of one argument inside one community, and she is the person holding
> the losing side of it.

So the parasite's branches fork on her authority. **Shore her up and the
observance happens; undercut her and the riot does** — and undercutting her
requires no invention at all, because the material is already there and already
true.

##### The personal front is a weapon the young already hold

She slept with the developer, or near enough that it has never been settled. She
raised his daughter through formative summers while the land fight was running.
The young do not need the parasite to hand them that; they need someone to say
it out loud at the wrong moment.

> **The most effective thing you can do to her costs almost nothing and involves
> no lies.** That is the whole amplification model in one character: the
> distribution already contains it, and volatility is rising on its own.

#### The practice is technique, not spirituality

Through sustained meditation and a precisely handled psychoactive preparation
she makes **direct contact with her guardian spirit** — and she is rigorous
about it, because she uses what she gets. She knows the plant's dosage and its
margins. She knows which of her own readings have held up over thirty years and
which have not. **She is her own harshest sceptic about the interpretation**,
and has to be: she makes decisions that affect people with it.

A symbiote has no ego, no interiority and nothing to say, so this is not a
dialogue and never reads as one. It is a **communion**: her own processing
altered until she can read what the thing living in her is holding — a share of
[the symbiotes' distributed store](ECOSYSTEM.md#the-network-is-also-a-store).

> **Discipline for every scene of this.** She is not told anything. Nothing
> speaks. She reads sediment — pressure, familiarity, wrongness, the shape of
> who is connected to whom — and renders it into the only language she has for
> it, which is the language of the tradition. **The translation is hers. The
> errors are hers too, and she knows that better than the player does.**

It should also be **unpleasant and expensive.** Hours of preparation, real
physical cost, days to recover, and she schedules it the way you schedule a
procedure — not often, and for a reason. If it is beautiful and effortless it
becomes the mystical-indigene cliché the rest of this section is built to avoid.

> **Compare the station.** A spectrograph is also special knowledge, trained
> tooling and the resolve to sit with a difficult instrument. Write her exactly
> at that register and the whole problem disappears.

#### She is the only instrument on the island that can see you

This is the mechanical payload and it is substantial.

| | sees | scope | latency |
|---|---|---|---|
| [the biologist](#the-biologist--who-can-detect-you) | a statistical anomaly | the station's data | slow, needs a conversation to assemble |
| [the astronomer](#the-astronomer--who-can-detect-your-predecessors) | the sites of your predecessors | centuries | irrelevant to the present |
| **the shaman in communion** | **deviation, directly** | **the whole contact network** | **as fast as she chooses to sit down** |

She has no idea it is a general-purpose sensor. She experiences it as the
guardian being troubled, which is the correct reading rendered in the wrong
vocabulary — and it points at you with no instrument, no data and no delay.

**So the person who controls access to the best event on the island is also the
person most able to catch you.** Those are not two characters. That is the whole
problem in one woman, and the player will want her for both reasons at once.

##### And she uses it for politics

She does not sit down to commune in order to protect the island from an unnamed
visitor. **She does it before negotiations** — to know which households are
leaning where, who has been in whose house, where the pressure in her own
community is building. It is an intelligence tool and she uses it like one.

> Her detection of you is therefore **incidental.** She goes looking for the
> shape of a land fight and finds something in the data she cannot place. That
> is a much better way for the player to be discovered than by someone hunting
> them, and it keeps her motives entirely her own.

#### She may already know about the hotel owner

A symbiote left her household on a death, during the summers the developer's
daughter spent there. Signatures are identities; the network holds them.

> **She may have sat down, reached in, and found one of her household's
> guardians answering from inside the colonizer's daughter.**

That upgrades [the family's account of
it](#the-family-may-have-an-account-of-it) from grief and superstition to
something she *read*. She cannot prove it, cannot explain it to anyone outside
the tradition, and cannot say it to the woman herself. It has been sitting
between them, unspoken, for twenty years.

It also means the estrangement has a cause neither of them has ever named, and
that the matriarch's wariness was never only political.

- **[open]** Whether she knows. The version where she does is much stronger and
  much crueller. The version where she suspects and has never dared look is
  available too.

#### Riding her is the game's thesis in one act

Inhabit the shaman, sit through the communion, and **you get read access to the
island's distributed store**: who holds what, which households are connected,
where every old symbiote on the island sits. It is the single richest
information source in the game.

It is also: a parasite using a woman's ceremonial office, her tradition's
hard-kept knowledge and her body's tolerance for a plant, to extract the one
thing her people managed to hold onto.

> **Every other faction on this island took land, labour, image or souls. This
> is the design's only route to taking the thing they successfully defended.**

And there is a second cost that is not about her at all. **She is the thing
holding her community's position together**, badly and under pressure from
below. Every expenditure on her spends down the authority that keeps the
[observance](#the-ceremony-is-an-invitation) possible — so the most direct route
to the island's knowledge runs through the one person whose stability the best
ending depends on. **Taking what she knows and getting what she runs are
rivalrous uses of the same woman.**

And it is dangerous in the obvious way: you would be inside a host whose entire
faculty is noticing you. **[open]** Whether communion-while-ridden detects the
rider — it almost has to, and the question is only whether she can tell what she
is detecting.

- **[open]** Whether the player can learn the practice and perform it in another
  host, or whether it is hers alone. Hers alone is cleaner and keeps her
  necessary.

### What the community wants from the eclipse

Independent of you, and it must be in play before you arrive: **the observance
is the largest thing they do**, it is attached to land they are fighting over,
and the island's other factions all want the same night for their own purposes.
A telescope-driven public viewing, a developer's party for twelve, a church
service, a cult rite and a riot-shaped resentment all land on one date.

The competition for the eclipse is a **land and recognition fight** before it is
anything to do with the parasite — which is what makes it usable, and what makes
using it the thing it is.

## Cast — unsorted

> **Raw character material.** Not yet reconciled with each other or with the
> faction structure. Mechanical consequences noted where they are already clear.
>
> **Everything here is subject to [the standing rule on
> characters](ECOSYSTEM.md#nobody-is-a-caricature-and-nobody-is-only-an-archetype):**
> each of these people is doing the best they believe they can achieve in a
> difficult situation, their one-line handle is a navigation aid rather than a
> description, and none of their views may be derivable from their faction.

### The old priest

Fire and brimstone. He preaches the devil, demons, temptation, Sodom and
Gomorrah — **demons that drive people to unspeakable deeds *en masse***,
cryptically referencing the island's past.

He calls the eclipse a sign of God turning away, the time of the devil, and
chastises the scientists for masking the fear and terrible awe of such an event
with their silly explanations. He hates the science community.

#### He rarely says mass because he was eased out, not because he cannot

**Correction to an earlier draft, which had him simply diminished.** He is
entirely capable. **The diocese pushed him aside** — gently, over years, without
anything being said plainly — after **complaints about his teaching.** It does
not align with doctrine, and it is far too robust for modern sensibilities.
Parishioners complained. The complaints were reasonable.

**That is why the [young priest](#the-young-priest) is here.** A cardinal with
responsibility for the diocese brought him in, and everyone involved understands
what that means, and nobody has ever said it out loud.

> **He was not retired for being wrong about the world. He was retired for being
> impossible in a parish**, which is a different thing and a far more ordinary
> one. No conspiracy removed him. A complaints process did.

##### The irony is exact

What the diocese hears as eccentric, doctrinally loose, embarrassingly vivid
preaching **is [the core's](#the-core-is-not-a-corruption-of-the-society-it-is-its-original-purpose)
content coming through the pulpit.** He never stopped saying it. He was simply
moved somewhere it could not be heard.

> **He was sidelined for the one thing about him that happens to be true** —
> and the institution that did it was acting reasonably on every piece of
> information it had.

##### He still has one room where he is listened to properly

He gives the society's gatherings **no-holds-barred services**: everything the
parish complained about, undiluted, to people who want it.

And this is the texture that keeps the whole thing human — **most of them are
not believers.** For the not-so-devout membership the old man's services are
**part of the thrill of belonging.** They have the taste of something forbidden.
You come away a little rattled and you tell nobody, and that is exactly the
point.

| who is in that room | why they are there |
|---|---|
| the core | they mean every word |
| most of the membership | **transgression, and the pleasure of being inside something** |
| the old priest | **the only place left where he is taken seriously** |

> So the boundary between the society and its core is **not a wall. It is a
> gradient of how seriously you take it** — which is how these things actually
> work, and it removes the last of the horror-trope framing.

##### Three consequences

- **The core's rite has an audience**, which matters for the arithmetic. It is
  not six men in a cellar; it is six men and everyone who came for the frisson.
  Headcount is higher than a cult gathering should be, the intensity is genuine
  because transgression genuinely arouses, and **nobody present was coerced into
  anything.**
- **Thrill converts to belief under pressure, with no help from you.** As the
  island tightens toward totality, some fraction of the people who came for the
  taste of it will stop treating it as a taste. That escalation is an entirely
  human mechanism — the parasite can only accelerate what the eclipse was going
  to do anyway.
- **He is a man who gave his life to an institution that has quietly told him he
  is an embarrassment**, and who has one room left. Per [the standing rule on
  characters](ECOSYSTEM.md#nobody-is-a-caricature-and-nobody-is-only-an-archetype),
  that is the whole of his motivation and it is sufficient.

#### He heads the society, and its core

> **Correction to an earlier draft, which made this far too sinister.** Secret
> societies, lodges, sects and orders are **ordinary**, especially in rural
> places. They concentrate power and therefore raise the risk of abuse of the
> powerless — but so do chambers of commerce, parish councils and golf clubs.
> **For most people on this island, the local order is not meaningfully
> different from a country club.** Write it that way.

**"The cult" is two things at two radii**, and most of the design's uses of the
word have meant only one of them.

| | **the society** (outer) | **the core** (inner) |
|---|---|---|
| who | most of the island's elders, across every powerful faction | the old priest and a handful of serious members |
| what it is | a social institution — dues, dinners, charity drives, bad speeches, introductions, contracts, a seat at a table | genuinely occult practice, pursued in earnest |
| how secret | **not really.** Everyone knows it exists and roughly who is in it. The content of meetings is private the way a board's is private. | **actually hidden**, including from the society around it |
| what it does to outsiders | the ordinary exclusion of any elite club: you are not in the room where things are decided | nothing visible at all |
| how it reads | dull | embarrassing, to the outer members, if they saw it — *old Father X takes it rather literally* |

##### The core is not a corruption of the society. It is its original purpose.

This is the part worth keeping. The society was founded for what the core still
does. The body around it **drifted into being a lodge** — kept the form, the
calendar and the standing, and quietly let the content go.

> **The sincere ones are the authentic ones.** The ordinary membership is the
> deviation. That inverts the usual shape of this trope, where a respectable
> institution turns out to have a dark secret: here a serious institution turned
> out to have a respectable majority.

Christian in shape, occult in practice: they stave off the reign of Satan by
**placating him** with old rites. The ordinary member hears that as inherited
language and a slightly odd annual observance. The core means it, and the
tension ramps as totality approaches. There are references to **a human
sacrifice in the island's dark past** — which the society treats as local
folklore and the core does not.

##### Three mechanical jobs, now assigned to the right radius

- **The society is a network and a venue.** It is made of elders, which means
  the oldest, widest-scoped symbiotes and the best-connected nodes in
  propagation — all of them in one room on a schedule. Valuable to the parasite
  as a contact graph long before it is interesting as a conspiracy.
- **The core is a gathering with a protocol**, escalating toward totality: a
  ready-made [transcendence event](MECHANICS.md#the-transcendence-event)
  configuration, narrow and self-selected, and the natural vehicle for the
  darkest route.
- **The past sacrifice is a previous eclipse.** The core's practices are garbled
  operational memory — placating a devil that was a parasite, with rites that may
  once have worked for reasons nobody retained. **The core is therefore the only
  group of humans on the island actively preparing for what is actually coming**,
  for entirely wrong reasons, and the most likely to recognise it when it
  arrives.

##### The exclusion is real, and it is not special

The matriarch is outside this power structure and it weakens her. **So is
everyone else who is outside it** — the young, the station's juniors, the
incomers, the poor, most of the island. That is what a concentration of power
does, and it needs no occult explanation to be a genuine political fact.

> Do not let the society carry moral weight it has not earned. It is a
> mechanism of exclusion of exactly the ordinary kind. **The design's critique
> of it is the same critique it makes of the development, the station and the
> hotel — and it does not need to be a devil-worship story to land.**

##### It is doing the same thing the islanders are doing

**This is the key to writing it without caricature.** The society is a group of
people **trying to preserve an old identity in a present that has no use for
it** — which is precisely [the native community's
project](#the-native-community), described in the same words.

| | **the community** | **the society** |
|---|---|---|
| the project | keep the tradition alive, adapted to the 21st century | keep the order alive, adapted to the 21st century |
| what it descends from | a real encounter with the entity at past eclipses | **the same encounter** |
| what it kept | the **practice** — intact, communal, performed in full | the **standing** — the calendar, the form, the seat |
| what it let go | the power, which it never had | the content, which survives only in the core |
| the young | find harmonisation an inadequate answer to injustice | find the whole thing an embarrassment and a racket |
| position | **outside** the power structure | **is** the power structure |

> **Same project, opposite ends of the island.** One preserves an identity with
> nothing behind it but the effort of the people doing it; the other preserves
> one backed by property, contracts and standing. **That difference is the
> entire moral distance between them — and it is a difference of position, not
> of sincerity.**

And they are **mirrored decay patterns of one original event.** Both traditions
began in a real encounter with something that arrived at totality. The community
kept the ceremony and lost nothing of it. The society kept the institution and
lost the ceremony to a handful of men who still mean it.

##### Which makes the old priest and the matriarch counterparts

They are in **structurally identical positions**: an elder holding a
preservation project together, arguing its worth to a younger generation that
has stopped finding it convincing, with a personal compromise in their past and
a deadline coming.

> They would understand each other immediately and will almost certainly never
> say so. **[proposal]** One scene of mutual recognition between them, late,
> brief, and resolving nothing, is worth more than any amount of explanation of
> either institution.

It also humanises him substantially without softening the critique by a single
degree: he is still at the centre of an exclusionary power structure, and he is
still doing the thing the game treats sympathetically when she does it.

#### He has been suppressing his own symbiote for fifty years

**[strong]** He is partly aware of it. He reads its nudges toward *complacent
collectivity* as demons trying to seduce him, and answers with self-denial and
self-harm — flagellation among them. **It works.**

> **He has independently invented the drain attack and dressed it as theology.**
> Symbiotes regulate toward homeostasis, and [forcing one to act costs
> it](ECOSYSTEM.md#their-restraint-is-costly-to-them). Persistent self-harm is
> anti-homeostatic: the symbiote spends itself correcting, continuously, for
> decades.

He is not wrong that something is whispering. He has only misidentified it — and
the thing he calls seduction toward the world is a regulator asking him to
remain viable.

**This gives him a target profile nothing else in the design has.** The symbiote
is the bridge, so a starved symbiote is a degraded bridge:

| | |
|---|---|
| **bandwidth to him** | **poor** — clumsy to operate, expensive to read |
| **resistance from him** | **low** — his defences are exhausted |

Cheap to push, hard to use. An elder with standing who should carry one of the
island's strongest symbiotes, and has spent his life crippling it without ever
knowing what it was.

### The hotel owner

Daughter of a powerful New York real-estate developer.

#### Her father, and what he left on the island

Through shady dealing he acquired protected coastal land and spent decades
pursuing permission to develop it. Individual small projects went through,
always against massive islander opposition.

**His breakthrough was a government contract to build part of the research
facilities on his land** — which finally gave him roads, water and electricity.
He also built the structures for the telescope, which is *not* on his land but
on the mountaintop: **a national park, and holy ground for the local tribes.**

> **This compromises the station.** It is not neutral infrastructure. It exists
> because of a land deal against indigenous interests, and its best instrument
> stands on sacred ground. The islanders' grievance therefore runs at the
> scientists too, the [young priest's](#the-young-priest) bridging becomes far
> harder and more impressive, and the research-ethics theme acquires a physical
> instance: the building they work in.

The great development never happened — resistance never let up. He lost
interest and left his properties to associates, **some of them local.** (Which
keeps the faction map from resolving into clean opposition: islanders are inside
the developer's structure, complicit and benefiting.)

##### He was in the society

**And so is basically everyone powerful on the island, except the natives —
with exceptions.** Which makes his membership **unremarkable**: of course he
was. It is a fact about his social class, not a revelation about his character,
and nothing follows from it on its own.

> **[The society](#he-heads-the-society-and-its-core) is not a superstition
> circle and it is not a conspiracy. It is the establishment** — the ordinary
> kind. Membership is how standing is held and transmitted here. Most of what
> happens at it is dinner.

Three consequences:

- **The [old priest](#the-old-priest) is not a crank with a secret.** He holds a
  respectable institutional position, and his contempt for the scientists
  carries weight for that reason rather than an occult one.
- **Whether the father was anywhere near the core is a separate question, and
  probably no.** Keeping him an ordinary member is better: his daughter inherits
  a social connection, not an initiation — a membership card, socially real and
  spiritually empty. **[open]**
- **"With exceptions" is co-option.** Those exceptions are almost certainly the
  locals inside the developer's structure — his associates. Entry has a price.
  Which fractures the indigenous faction internally, and should: a monolithic
  indigenous community would be weak writing, and a wound running through it is
  truer.

##### He collected fossils, hunted, and kept an amateur naturalist's eye

**The husks are already an unclassifiable taxon in the scientific record. A
fossil collector working this island would have them.**

> **There is a cabinet in that villa, and the hotel owner owns it.** The
> problematic fossil the station's biologist could identify, and the devil the
> cult has placated for generations, are **the same objects** — in a private
> house belonging to a woman who does not know what either of them is.

The two exposition channels meet in one dead man's collection.

It also makes him a man who held every piece: the fossils, the society, the
wildlife, the land, the money. **Whether he ever assembled them should stay
ambiguous** — better than either answer.

> And it gives the [society/core
> distinction](#he-heads-the-society-and-its-core) a job. An ordinary member
> hears the annual language about placating a devil as inherited nonsense and
> thinks nothing of it. **A fossil collector hears the same sentence with a
> cabinet of unclassifiable husks in the next room.** He did not need to be an
> initiate to be the one man positioned to notice — which is a better reason for
> him to have gone quiet about it than membership would be.

#### Her

She spent the summers of her formative years on the island while the first
builds were going through, restoring and extending a colonial-style villa that
became a family summer home. **All her best memories are from here.** She loves
the island deeply, and it has fought her family for as long as she can remember.

After her father's death, and **a brutal, deeply scarring inheritance fight**
with her siblings, she came away with the island holdings — by far the most
commercially fragile part of the estate.

She tried to reconcile: sustainable eco-tourism, alternative energy, organic
farming. But for all the Ivy League education she has neither the business
instinct nor **her father's ruthlessness** in handling his old associates. Most
of the inheritance is gone, the land heavily mortgaged, little to show.

**The super-exclusive eclipse party was her [obnoxious
brother's](#the-brothers) idea**, and he supplied the first guests — two
Californian crypto bros. The list has grown to twelve. It is her last chance to
turn the business around and stay on the island she feels bound to.

> **And the failure it is meant to rescue was partly engineered by him.** She
> attributes it to lacking her father's instinct. Her brother has had a hand on
> the scale for years, through the old local staff who are loyal to him
> personally. **He built the hole and then sold her the ladder.**

#### The indigenous family

Several of them worked at her father's properties, which is how she met them.
**The matriarch took her in.** She learned the island from that household, and
there she **fell in love for the first time — hard.**

Her father allowed it; he was consumed with the development. **And there had
been a prior connection between him and the matriarch** — possibly romantic.

The fractiousness of the politics eventually estranged her from the indigenous
community. **She retains tenuous ties to that one family**, which is an
important one.

##### Two decisions to make deliberately

- **[open] How far the father/matriarch connection went.** The timeline permits
  a child, which would make her first love a half-sibling. That is a register
  change rather than a detail — it pulls toward family melodrama and away from
  the quieter political tragedy. The two versions want different scenes.
- **[open] Whether the first love is still on the island.** Estrangement by
  politics implies they are. If so, that is a live wire beside a woman who is
  broke, desperate and hosting crypto investors on contested land. Real feeling,
  unresolved, on both sides — **the design's ideal amplification material,
  because none of it has to be manufactured.**

Either way the connection means **the land fight was personal on both sides.**
The matriarch's opposition is not only politics, and the father's eventual loss
of interest may have had causes nobody names.

#### She is the only one in her family with a symbiote

**[proposal] It came from the matriarch's household.** Years of close contact,
taken in as family, during formative years — the transmission profile exactly.
Symbiotes move on host death to someone they have built a driver for, and
someone in that house died during those summers.

> **It went to the developer's daughter instead of to their own kin.** An
> important lineage lost one permanently, at a timescale where it cannot be
> replaced. One more thing her family took and never knew about — including her.

##### The family may have an account of it

**[proposal]** The [old priest](#the-old-priest) has partial awareness of his
own; indigenous tradition plausibly does too. So there may be a belief in that
house that **something which should have stayed, went away** while she was
there.

> Which reads to everyone, themselves included, as superstition, grief and
> ordinary resentment of the developer's daughter. And is literally true.

It gives the estrangement a layer none of them can articulate and the player can
eventually see — and it means their wariness of her was never only political.

##### Mechanically, she is a door

An important indigenous household means old symbiotes, dense contact and a tight
cluster: **the hardest group on the island to reach.** And she has ties straight
into it, built on genuine love and genuine loss.

> The parasite's cheapest route to the island's most protected people runs
> through the one relationship she has left. **Using her affection as the entry
> point is both the obvious play and the thing the game is about.**

##### She is the exact inverse of the young priest

| | the priest | the hotel owner |
|---|---|---|
| socially | embraced by every faction | resented, with cause, for generations |
| symbiotically | **foreign** — signature will not resolve for centuries | **native** — an island symbiote, fully in the network |
| acting on them | loud locally, **silent globally** | ordinary locally, **loud across the whole island** |

He belongs to the people and not to the network. She belongs to the network and
not to the people. Neither knows, and the thing inside her is more at home than
she will ever be.

As a pair they cover the two ways of being an outsider here and invert each
other's strategic profile exactly.

#### She is the hub of the endgame

Everything converges on one person, because her father touched all of it and she
inherited the wreckage:

| thread | how it reaches her |
|---|---|
| **the scientists** | the fossil collection is in her house |
| **the wealthy visitors** | her party, twelve of them |
| **the indigenous family** | tenuous ties, genuine love, genuine loss |
| **the cult** | her father's membership, however loosely it transfers |
| **the symbiote network** | hers came from the matriarch's household and is fully resolved |
| **[her brother](#the-brothers)** | he manufactured the failure, supplied the guests, and is using her venue |

A lot of load for one character, but coherent rather than contrived: she is the
hub precisely because she is the one left holding what he built.

**[proposal]** She may have **childhood fragments she never interpreted** —
gatherings at the villa, things seen as a girl and filed as adult strangeness.
Worth keeping: it lets her be brought to understanding without anyone explaining
anything.

#### The party makes the mid-game concrete

Twelve guests is far too small to be a [transcendence
event](MECHANICS.md#the-transcendence-event) — but it clarifies the problem,
because **it is not the only gathering.** The cult has its rite, the islanders
their observance, the station a public viewing, and she has a party for the very
rich.

> The mid-game stops being *cause an event* and becomes **several gatherings
> already exist, competing for the same sky — merge them, or swell one.**

##### And twelve was never the real headcount

[Her brother's actual plan](#the-event) brings in **a large number of young
islanders**, procured. So the party is not a dozen rich people in a room; it is
a dozen rich people and everyone they have arranged to have there.

**She has been told a guest number and is hosting an event.** The gap between
those two facts is most of her character's arc.

### The brothers

**Three children, not two.** The eldest, a middle brother, and [the hotel
owner](#the-hotel-owner), who is much the youngest.

| | |
|---|---|
| **the eldest** | runs most of the father's empire. Groomed for it from childhood. The subject of this section. |
| **the middle** | bought out by the eldest. Keeps as much distance as he can manage, and is in **occasional contact with his sister, of whom he is fond.** |
| **the sister** | came away from the inheritance fight with the island holdings — the most commercially fragile part of the estate. |

#### The eldest

**He was the successor from the beginning**, trained harshly, carrying the full
weight of his father's expectations for his entire life. He did the work. He is
extremely good at it. The empire is intact because of him.

He **despises his younger siblings** — the sister most, because she is so much
younger and had the easiest ride of all — and he **pities them**, genuinely and
without contradiction, for their lack of ambition, competence and ruthlessness.
Both feelings are sincere and he holds them at once.

> **He is not a caricature and the standing rule applies to him hardest.** Write
> the scene where he explains what it cost to be the one who had to be capable,
> to a sister who got the island and resents him for everything else. **He is
> right about the facts and monstrous about the conclusion**, and the scene must
> let him be right.

##### He has been sabotaging her, and she does not know

Through **his father's old local staff** — men he has known since he was a boy
actively helping run the early building projects, who are loyal to him
personally in a way they were never loyal to the father.

That is a **second shadow power structure on the island**, parallel to [the
society](#he-heads-the-society-and-its-core) and invisible in the same way: not
a faction, not an institution, just a set of men who will take a call. The
matriarch is not in that one either.

> **Her commercial failure is partly manufactured.** She reads it as her own
> lack of her father's instinct — the self-assessment she already carries — and
> the truth is that her brother has had a hand on the scale for years.

**This is the best secret in the design for [information
brokerage](MECHANICS.md#4-information-brokerage--between-humans).** It is true,
it is devastating, it is held by nobody who will ever tell her, and delivering
it to her changes what she does. The parasite is the only thing that can carry
it.

##### He inherited the father's assembled picture

The love/hate with his father was intense and he was **trusted with information
from an early age** — including the father's fascination with the eclipse events
and the lore around them.

And [the father was uniquely placed](#he-collected-fossils-hunted-and-kept-an-amateur-naturalists-eye),
with three independent angles on it:

| angle | what it gave him |
|---|---|
| **[society](#he-heads-the-society-and-its-core) membership** | an ordinary member, but an important one — the rites, the placation language, the island's dark past as the order tells it |
| **close ties to the research station** | the fossils, the anomalies, the eclipse track |
| **close ties to the [matriarch](#the-matriarch)** | the guardian spirits, the trickster, the ceremony |

> **Whether the father ever assembled them stays ambiguous — but he told his
> son.** The son assembled it, and reached the wrong conclusion. That resolves
> the father's ambiguity without spending it, and it puts the only assembled
> picture on the island in the hands of the person least equipped to be careful
> with it.

##### What he took from the lore

Not the cosmology. **The orgies.** The myths of wild excess, the nights of
legendary abandon, the sanctioned collapse of every rule — that is the part that
caught him, and he has been fascinated by it for years.

A man who was never allowed to be anything but disciplined, reading inherited
accounts of institutionalised licence. It is not subtle and it does not need to
be.

#### The event

Over the last few years he and **a couple of ultra-rich men in his circle** have
been planning a **private event during the eclipse** — a deliberate attempt to
engineer one of the legendary nights for themselves.

The venue is **his sister's hotel**, which he reached by way of the weakness he
spent years creating. [The party was his
idea](#her); he supplied the first guests; the list is now twelve.

> **He manufactured her desperation, then sold her the solution to it.** She
> thinks it is her last chance to save the business. It is.

##### Who is supposed to be there

Him and **the one or two who are actually in on it** — see [the
tourists](#the-tourists), where most of the guest list has no idea and would
object — and **a large number of young women, and some men, from the island.**

The plan involves **coercion of every available kind**: money, debt, employment,
leverage on families, immigration status, whatever is to hand. And it does not
trouble any of them in the slightest, because **they hold it as their right.**
Being rich and powerful simply means that manipulating and using people who are
neither is available to you, the way a tool is available.

> **This is the human layer's parasite**, stated as plainly as the design will
> ever state it. He reads people, finds what constrains them, reaches into a
> distribution he did not create, and takes what he came for. The only
> differences are that he chose it, he enjoys it, and he has a theory about why
> he is entitled.

#### What this does to the design

##### A human has already done your mid-game

The parasite's [central project](MECHANICS.md#the-mid-to-late-arc-is-engineering-one)
is engineering a mass emotional event. **He has been working on his for years,
better resourced, with a venue, a guest list and lead time.**

Which gives the design most of what [a rival
seed](DESIGN.md#open-questions) would have given it — a competing planner, a
clock, a reason to hurry — at a fraction of the cost, and without a second
non-human agent.

##### The plan cannot work

**It is not a dark route. It is a crime with no payoff, and the design should be
explicit about that.**

Two independent reasons, either sufficient:

1. **The island will stop it.** Almost everyone will be heavily against it the
   moment they understand, and many before — [the baseline defeats
   it](#in-the-baseline-it-fails).
2. **Even if they did not, it yields nothing.** [The field forms among the
   many](MECHANICS.md#the-field-forms-among-the-many-and-that-decides-everything):
   a dozen exhilarated men and dozens of coerced people in terror is a room
   where **the majority is outside the field entirely.** Perfect suppression of
   all resistance still ends in an event with no yield.

> **So the best case for the brother is an event that amounts to mass sexual
> assault and achieves nothing whatsoever.** No transcendence, no fabled
> outcome, nothing. That is worth stating plainly in the design, because it
> removes the possibility of the darkest content also being the optimal play.

##### What it can cause is the real danger

**[The backlash](MECHANICS.md#the-darkest-route-collective-rage-fuelled-by-what-it-does).**
Once it becomes apparent what was being arranged, the young of this island have
a true grievance, a named target and a date — and the ratio inverts: **a great
many enraged islanders, a few terrified outsiders.**

> **The brother's plan is not a route. It is a fuse.** Its value to a dark
> player is not that it happens but that it is *found out*, late, publicly, at
> maximum volatility.

Which inverts the dark play around it completely: the player does not suppress
the opposition to his plan. They **let the truth arrive at the hour of their
choosing** — and the atrocity that follows is against the outsiders, committed
by the wronged, architected by the only non-human thing present.

##### In the baseline, it fails

**Correction to a first reading of this character, which had the event as a
free atrocity the player merely declines to prevent.** That is wrong, and the
reason is the design's own baseline.

> [In the untouched timeline most of the island ends up at the low-key music
> festival](MECHANICS.md#the-baseline-is-the-proof). A plan this harmful to
> islanders generates the maximum the community can generate, and **symbiote
> resistance in practice means people being mobilised against it.**

Mothers make calls. Staff talk. The young priest hears something and will not
let it go. The matriarch finds out and has every reason and every channel to
act. **It does not survive contact with a community that notices.**

##### And if it collapses he goes to the core

He does not leave the island. **[He is one of the few people here who believes
something could actually happen](#if-it-collapses-the-brother-does-not-go-home)**,
so a failed party routes him to the only other group that believes it — which
means breaking his event converts a dark route rather than closing one.

##### And his apparatus is the one thing worth taking from him

The plan is worthless; **the logistics are not.** He has assembled a drug
supply, costumes, money, a guest list of people who will attend anything, and a
date.

[Redirected to the beach](MECHANICS.md#the-other-viable-high-intensity-event-is-the-beach),
with the hippies who supplied him, the young islanders and — hardest — the
native community, all of it becomes **the one high-intensity event in the game
that actually works**, for the single reason that everybody there chose to come.

> **The same materials build the worthless atrocity and the viable
> celebration.** What separates them is not scale, intensity or taste. It is
> whether the people in the room were asked.

##### He is not a cheap host. He is barely a host at all.

> **Correction.** An earlier reading called him the cheapest host in the game,
> on the grounds that everything the player wants is already near the top of his
> distribution. **That reasoning ignores the bridge.**

[Symbiotes are the medium](MECHANICS.md#they-are-the-bridge) through which the
parasite perceives and acts on a human at all. **He has none.** So the ordinary
operations are simply unavailable:

| | on anyone else | on him |
|---|---|---|
| read his state | yes | **no** |
| nudge him | yes | **no** |
| jump into him | yes | **no** |
| act on him | through his symbiote | **only through other people** |

He is not an efficient instrument. **He is an opaque object in the middle of the
board**, and everything the player ever does to him has to be done by moving
somebody else.

##### The only route in is a direct link, and it is very narrow

[Direct links from outside](MECHANICS.md#5-eviction--the-hard-power-route)
require two things the brother conspicuously does not offer:

| requirement | why he fails it |
|---|---|
| **deep intimate knowledge** — the driver | he is barely on the island, guarded, and known well by almost nobody |
| **extended physical contact** — the bandwidth | he is an estranged, controlled adult man who does not touch people for long |

**One person clears the first requirement.** [His sister](#the-hotel-owner) has
lifelong knowledge of him and is **next of kin** — and kinship is exactly the
condition that makes driver-building tractable, by the same mechanism that lets
[a symbiote relocate into a
relative](ECOSYSTEM.md#how-symbiotes-persist-between-eclipses).

**Nobody clears the second.** Estranged adult siblings do not hold each other
for long enough, and no amount of nudging manufactures an occasion for it.

##### Unless he breaks

There is one circumstance in which this man would be held for a long time by the
only person who knows him well enough: **a genuine collapse, and it would be
about his father.**

The love/hate, the harsh training, the lifetime of carrying someone else's
expectations — that is the real wound, and it is intact. If it opens while his
sister is the person present, she will comfort him. **She is the kind of person
who would, and that is precisely what makes it available.**

> **To reach the man who manufactured her ruin, the player has to engineer a
> moment of real tenderness between them** — and then use her arms around him as
> a cable. There is no cleaner statement of what this game is.

##### Which makes it a project, not a move

Spelled out, the path requires all of:

1. **Holding the sister**, and a driver for her good enough to steer a hard
   conversation
2. **The father material** — the lore, the training, whatever is in that villa —
   because the lever is specific and the player has to have found it
3. **An actual breakdown.** It cannot be faked. [You can only amplify what is
   there](MECHANICS.md#what-the-parasite-actually-does), so the player must reach
   far enough into the distribution of **the most defended man on the island** to
   surface collapse. That is one of the most expensive single pushes in the game.
4. **Repeated or sustained contact**, because a direct link from outside is slow.
   Probably not one embrace — a long night, or several occasions.
5. **A sister not so poisoned against him that she walks out.** Which the player
   may already have spent, if they told her [what he
   did](#he-has-been-sabotaging-her-and-she-does-not-know).

That last item is the good one: **the brokerage payload and the possession route
are rivalrous.** Tell her the truth and you lose the only bridge to him. Keep it
and she stays in the dark for your convenience.

> **Very narrow, and worth keeping narrow.** As a reliable option it would make
> him a tool. As a difficult, late, specific achievement it is one of the best
> things the player can do — and it ends with a [direct link to a
> gap](#he-has-no-symbiote), which is the one kind that survives the shell.

##### It is the riot's ignition, and the sharpest one available

Young islanders procured for rich men's eclipse party is not an abstract
grievance about historical exploitation. It is **a thing happening this week, to
named people, with a date.**

So [the generational axis](#the-generational-axis), [the riot
branch](#it-is-the-way-into-the-riot-branch) and the brother's event are **one
thread, not three.** The young priest hears about it in confession. The
matriarch hears about it from a mother. Everything converges.

##### He has no symbiote

A [gap in the world](ECOSYSTEM.md#how-symbiotes-persist-between-eclipses) —
which is a **mechanical fact about reach, not a moral one about his nature.**

Symbiotes do not restrain people; [they interfere
minimally](ECOSYSTEM.md#minimal-interference), so having one would not have
stopped him and its absence does not explain him. What it changes is that **the
island's regulatory system has no handle on him at all.**

> Resistance works by moving people. **He is the one person on the island who
> cannot be moved that way** — not because he is strong, but because there is
> nothing in him to move him through.

Three consequences:

- **It is part of why the plan got this far.** Everyone else involved has been
  getting a faint, unattributable reluctance for years. He never has.
- **The community can still stop him**, because the people he needs are not
  gaps. The opposition routes through the staff, the families and the young
  islanders he is counting on — which is exactly where the player has to work.
- **He is a direct-link candidate.** Gaps are [the harmless third
  route](ECOSYSTEM.md#how-symbiotes-persist-between-eclipses) to the only thing
  that outlives the shell. **The most harmful human on the island is the one
  the parasite can safely bond with permanently** — and a failed run that ends
  linked to him is an ending where you survive as the symbiote of a man you
  cannot restrain, which is the bleakest outcome available and entirely
  consistent with the rules.

#### Two things to settle

- **[open] How much the sister knows.** The strongest version is that she
  **half-knows**: she has been told entertainment is arranged, she has not
  asked, and she has noticed that she is not asking. Innocent makes her a victim
  twice over; fully complicit makes her unusable as the endgame hub. Half is
  both harsher and more human.
- **[open] What the middle brother is for.** He is the only member of the family
  with no stake, which is rare in this cast — but *he got out and said nothing
  for twenty years*, which makes his distance a failure rather than a virtue.
  The useful version is a man who could have warned her at any point and chose
  peace.

> **Writing caution, and it is the most serious one in the document.** The
> event's content is the most dangerous material in the design. It must be
> written from the position of the people being used and never from the
> position of the men using them — see [the
> register](#for-the-register) and [do not state the
> critique](ECOSYSTEM.md#do-not-state-the-critique). The coercion should be
> **administrative and boring** in its mechanics — a debt, a shift rota, a
> family member's job — because that is what it actually looks like.

### The tourists

**Structurally identical to [the society](#he-heads-the-society-and-its-core): an
outer body with a reason, and a small core with the real one.**

| | **the guests** (outer) | **the core** (inner) |
|---|---|---|
| who | twelve or so very rich people | [the brother](#the-eldest) and one or two others |
| why they came | **an exclusive eclipse event among their peers.** A rare sky, a private island, the right names on the list | **to engineer one of the legendary nights** out of the lore the brother inherited |
| what they know | that it will be a very good party | everything |
| what they do when it becomes obvious | **nothing. They enjoy it.** | it was always the point |

#### They are not conspirators, and they are not innocent

> **Correction to a first reading, which had most of the guest list objecting
> once they understood. They will not object.**

They are **entirely comfortable with the exploitation of the island and the
islanders**, on one condition: that it is in the service of their pleasure and
they do not have to look at it directly. That is not an exception they make for
this event. It is how they travel.

**The sexualisation of the islanders will be obvious very early** — who was
hired, how they are dressed, what "local entertainment" is being used to mean.
Nobody will miss it.

> **And they will read it as *edgy*, or as *cute*.** A flavour, not a fact. The
> appalling thing is not that they fail to notice; it is that noticing is part
> of the appeal, and asking one further question is the only move unavailable to
> them.

#### They are the game's question, arriving pre-answered

**[How could you let this happen](MECHANICS.md#the-moral-question-changes-shape)
is the design's central moral question.** The player spends the whole game
engineering that state in other people, one ordinary hesitation at a time.

> **The tourists arrive in it already.** Nobody has to suppress them. Their
> entire social formation did it years ago, for free.

So they are not a resistance surface. **They are a demonstration of the end
state** — a picture of what the player is manufacturing everywhere else, in
people who required no manufacturing at all. The player should recognise their
own work in a group they never touched.

- **[open]** Whether one guest is written as an exception. **Recommend at most
  one, and not a hero** — someone who leaves quietly, tells nobody, and is never
  heard from again. More than one, and the group's actual quality is lost.

#### The complicity is not a discount

They are complicit, in the ambient way — and that is precisely why it buys the
design nothing.

> **It is the kind of complicity nearly everyone in the story has.** Treating it
> as a licence for what happens to them would license treating most of the cast
> that way. [The standing
> rule](ECOSYSTEM.md#nobody-is-a-caricature-and-nobody-is-only-an-archetype)
> applies to them at full strength, and they are harder to write well than
> anyone except the brother.

#### Mechanically they are food

Dropping the resistance reading leaves a cleaner one. By [the compute/standing
split](#the-computestanding-split-still-holds) they are:

| | |
|---|---|
| cognition | high — educated, well-fed, unoccupied |
| community standing | **none.** Nobody here is theirs |
| symbiote protection | **weakest on the island** — recent arrivals, unknown signatures, no network integration |
| emotional volatility | **high and deliberately cultivated.** They came to feel something |

**High value, no defence, and already primed.** The least protected people at
the best-attended private event on the island.

#### If the orgy happens, they are used too

**The coercion gradient does not stop at the islanders.** The core's event
consumes everyone present at wildly different levels of power and consent:

- **some guests buy in enthusiastically**, and would describe it afterwards as
  the best night of their lives
- **some are swept**, in the ordinary way that a room's momentum sweeps people,
  and are not sure afterwards what happened to them
- **and all of them have recourse that no islander has** — money, lawyers,
  distance, and the option of never coming back

> That asymmetry in the *aftermath* is sharper than any asymmetry on the night,
> and it is the part worth writing. **The guest who was genuinely harmed will be
> fine. The islander who was will still live here.**

- **[open]** Whether a used guest becomes an aftermath thread. They are the only
  people harmed at that event with the power to do something about it — which
  makes them a late, unreliable and entirely self-interested route to
  consequences for the brother.

#### The two or three

Keep them distinct from each other, and from him:

- **one true peer** — equally rich, equally bored, in it for the same reason and
  without the brother's inherited obsession. He wants a story to tell.
- **one hanger-on** — in it for proximity to the brother, who would abandon the
  whole thing instantly if the brother did. **The weak link, and the player will
  find him.**

#### If it collapses, the brother does not go home

**This is the branch that makes the whole structure earn its place.**

The plan can fall apart several ways — [the island
resists](#in-the-baseline-it-fails), a guest balks, the sister finds out, the
player breaks it. In none of them does he simply leave.

> **He is one of the very few people on this island who believes something could
> actually happen at this eclipse** — not as a vibe, but because [his father
> told him things](#he-inherited-the-fathers-assembled-picture) from three
> independent directions and he put them together.

So he goes to **[the core](#the-core-is-not-a-corruption-of-the-society-it-is-its-original-purpose)**
— the only other group on the island that believes it too. The door is open:
his father was a member, his name is good, and he arrives with money,
conviction and a free week.

##### What that does — and what it does not

> **It does not make the rite happen.** An earlier reading had the collapse of
> the party automatically upgrading the core's rite into a dangerous event. That
> is the same mistake twice: **[the default is still the
> festival](MECHANICS.md#the-world-defaults-to-dispersal)**, for the tourists,
> the society, the islanders and the station alike.

In the untouched timeline he joins, they perform their rite, a dozen people
attend, most of the membership is at the festival with everyone else, and
**nothing happens.**

What it creates is a **potential**, which is a different and better thing:

- **Breaking his event is not a win. It is a branch.** The player does not
  remove a dark route; they relocate the one person on the island with money,
  conviction and nothing left to do, into the one group with an actual protocol.
  **Whether that becomes anything is entirely the player's work.**
- **The core gets a benefactor** — resources, energy, and someone young enough
  to carry it. That raises the *ceiling* of what the rite could be. It does not
  raise the floor.
- **The old priest is believed.** By a serious man, with standing, for the first
  time in years — after a lifetime of being told he is an embarrassment and
  [quietly removed for it](#he-rarely-says-mass-because-he-was-eased-out-not-because-he-cannot).
  It would transform him, and it should be written as a kindness that turns out
  to be a catastrophe **only if the player makes it one.**
- **And the father's three strands come home.** The assembled picture arrives
  inside the one group positioned to act on it.

##### Not a conversion

He does not find faith. **He redirects the same appetite.** He wanted the
legendary night; the core offers the legendary night with a theory attached, and
a better claim on it. Write it as a man upgrading his plan, not as a man
humbled.

##### And it gives the baseline its sting

The core has been correct in principle and wrong in practice for a century. The
rite is performed, badly attended, and nothing comes.

> **This is the year they are right** — and in the baseline they still get
> nothing, because the thing they are inviting spends the evening at a music
> festival with everybody else.

#### A caution about the pattern

This is the **second** group on the island built as *visible body, smaller real
reason*. There are exactly two:

| | how long the gap took to form | where the core sits |
|---|---|---|
| **[the society](#he-heads-the-society-and-its-core)** | **a century.** The body forgot | the core is the **original** purpose, surviving inside a body that drifted |
| **the tourists** | **eighteen months.** The body never knew | the core is the **origin**, and the body is the cover it built |

Same shape, opposite histories, wildly different timescales. Written that way it
reads as an observation about how these things form. Written carelessly it is
the same reveal twice.

> **The [native community](#the-native-community) is not an instance of this**,
> and should not be made into one. The matriarch's ceremonial role is **a public
> office with no concealed purpose** — everyone knows it exists and what it is
> for. What she has that others do not is skill, not secrecy.

##### Where the pattern would tip over

**[The station](#the-director) is the one that could make it a third**, and it
is deliberately built not to be. The director has a loyal group inside a public
institution, which is the right shape — but **nothing about it is concealed
except the through-line.** Every project is real, funded, published and
defensible; only the fact that a dozen of them are one project is unstated.

> A third *hidden core* would be a tic. **A third kind of hiding is a theme.**
> Keep the difference sharp, and do not build a fourth of anything.

### The old hippies

> Promoted from a [faction table](#who-lives-here) row, because the only viable
> orgiastic route in the game runs through them.

Settled here decades ago, on land they did not have a claim to, in pursuit of a
paradise they then spent forty years defending against everyone who arrived
after them — including their own children. The faction table has them wanting
**paradise preserved *on their terms*** and extracting **belonging in a place
that is not theirs**, which remains exactly right and is the hardest extraction
in the design to write honestly, because it is the gentlest and the most
sincerely meant.

#### They supplied the brother's drugs

**The concrete hook, and it is a good one.** Whatever is going to the hotel came
from the beach. They are **already materially inside a plan they would be
appalled by**, and they have no idea what it is for.

Which gives the player a lever that is simply the truth: tell them what their
supply was for. It costs nothing, it is accurate, and it detonates.

#### They are the bridge between the tourists and the island

Nobody else is. They are **white, foreign, and have been here forty years** —
not of the community and no longer of anywhere else. That makes them the only
group that can plausibly convene outsiders and islanders on the same sand,
which is the precondition for [the beach
route](MECHANICS.md#the-other-viable-high-intensity-event-is-the-beach).

> And it is the one occasion where their forty years of **not quite belonging**
> turns into a capability. They have spent decades being tolerated by both
> sides. Here that is worth something.

#### Their generational conflict is the cruellest one

| | |
|---|---|
| **the founders** | came for freedom, built a paradise, and now own the gate |
| **their children** | grew up here, are not islanders, are not visitors, and inherited an ideology that cannot explain why they are landlords |

The [generational axis](#the-generational-axis) version here is **children who
cannot even make the accusation cleanly**, because the parents agree with every
word of it in principle.

#### Two cautions

- **Not comic relief.** Ageing hippies are an easy laugh and the laugh costs the
  design everything it needs them for.
- **Their sincerity is the point.** They did mean it, they do still mean it, and
  they have been quietly profiting from a housing market and a land settlement
  they would denounce in any other country. **Both at once, with no irony
  available to them**, per [the standing
  rule](ECOSYSTEM.md#nobody-is-a-caricature-and-nobody-is-only-an-archetype).

- **[open]** Whether a founder is named and cast, or the group acts through its
  children. The beach route probably needs one founder with the standing to
  convene it, and one of the children with ties to the young islanders.

### The young priest

From Ghana. Young, highly educated, well travelled. He runs the parish day to
day and says most of the masses — **interdenominational services, well
attended.**

Deep links to the science community *and* the indigenous group, built on mutual
respect and fascination. He knows about [the
society](#he-heads-the-society-and-its-core) — everyone does — and is **wary of
it in the ordinary way a young clergyman is wary of the parish's old money.**
Of [the core](#the-core-is-not-a-corruption-of-the-society-it-is-its-original-purpose)
he knows nothing, though he has noticed that his superior is strange about the
eclipse.

**He is probably the most valuable person on the island.** Maximum connectivity
across every faction is exactly what [compute](MECHANICS.md#compute) wants, and
the design holds that central, well-connected people are the hardest targets. He
is the prize.

#### Familiarity and otherness

The eclipse folklore resonates deeply with his own past and culture, and he
reports **a strange sensation of extreme familiarity and extreme otherness** —
which he discusses openly with the scientists and with the shaman.

**[proposal]** **He is the only person who arrived on the island carrying a
symbiote** — one of a different lineage, from an African site where comparable
things happened far earlier.

##### Why it is very old, and what that costs

"Old" must mean **an unbroken relocation chain.** Symbiotes do not reproduce;
they move host to host at each death along kin lines, and failed transitions are
the sink. Persisting since the deep past means tens of thousands of successful
handovers without a single break.

> That is astronomically improbable, which makes his passenger **special by luck
> rather than by category** — not "African symbiotes are older" but *this
> particular thread never snapped.* There may be only a handful like it on
> Earth.

The underlying logic holds: Africa is where hominins became social-cognitive, so
it is where the substrate first appeared, where parasites first found purchase,
and where symbiotes first accumulated.

> **Liability, and the fix.** This is the [ancient-aliens
> problem](ECOSYSTEM.md#the-liability) aimed at human origins in Africa
> specifically — ground with an ugly history of pseudo-archaeology.
>
> **The symbiotes followed us; we did not follow them.** Humans became
> social-cognitive for ordinary evolutionary reasons. Parasites arrived
> *because* we became viable. Symbiotes accumulated as debris, and their
> presence then mildly reinforced a trend already underway. They are parasites —
> they do not create their hosts. The locus follows the population; the
> population is not chosen.

##### Maximum strength, scope of one

An ancient symbiote should be the strongest on the island — but scope is *the
community it regulates for*, and his knows nobody here.

> **Maximum resistance, minimum breadth.** He is individually the hardest person
> on the island to touch, and the community gets no protection from him at all.

No other strong symbiote has that profile; the rest are strong *and* broad.

**The reason is non-recognition, not ignorance.** Symbiotes identify each other
by signature over the body channel, and calibration takes centuries (see [They
recognise each other](ECOSYSTEM.md#they-recognise-each-other-and-they-form-a-network)).
His does not resolve to the island's network and never will.

> **He will be foreign for his entire life, however completely the humans take
> him in.** The congregation loves him, the scientists respect him, the
> indigenous community trusts him — and at a level none of them can perceive, he
> is not there at all.

**Which inverts his target profile.** He resists ferociously as an individual,
but his symbiote's alarm does not propagate, because the network cannot read a
signature it does not resolve.

> **Hardest to break into, quietest once you are in.** Loud locally, silent
> globally. He is not only the prize — he is the *safe* prize, for a player who
> can pay the entry cost.

##### Where the familiarity actually comes from

Not recognition: symbiotes are not agents and have no memory. But they carry
**drivers**, and a very old one carries an enormous library of them — models of
humans accumulated over an immense span.

> What he experiences as familiarity is **his own intuition being unusually
> good.** He reads people and situations with uncanny depth and has no idea why.
> It is also exactly why he is so effective at bridging the scientists, the
> indigenous group and the congregation.

The otherness is the dissonance: the island's specifics do not fit the models. A
constant low wrongness underneath an unearned fluency.

##### The temptation this creates

**[open]** If his symbiote is a driver repository, and **eviction inherits what
it held**, he becomes the single most valuable target in the game — a hundred
thousand years of human models taken in one act.

The most monstrous available move would also be the most efficient, performed on
the kindest person on the island, destroying the thing that makes him kind.

The design currently grants eviction *unmediated access*, not inheritance.
Adding inheritance is a significant buff and a very large story hook. Decide
deliberately.

#### The pair

Old and young, fire-and-brimstone and ecumenical, hating the scientists and
collaborating with them, secretly occult and openly curious, suppressing his
symbiote and carrying a foreign one.

**A Ghanaian priest serving a congregation with a colonial past is the mission
returning** — the island's whole history inverted inside one person.

##### He is also the man who replaced the old one

The cardinal sent him [in response to complaints about the old
priest](#he-rarely-says-mass-because-he-was-eased-out-not-because-he-cannot).
That is the relationship's foundation and **neither of them will ever mention
it.**

He runs the parish well, is loved for it, and is **the instrument of the old
man's humiliation** — not through anything he did, simply by being competent in
a job that used to be someone else's. Any kindness he shows is therefore
complicated at the source, and he knows it, which is why he is careful with him
in a way that reads to the old priest as pity.

> The old priest's contempt for the young one is **not ideological first.** It
> is the contempt of a man for his replacement, dressed up as a doctrinal
> objection because the doctrinal objection is available and true.

Both of them are doing the best they can see to do. Neither has a good option.

#### What he failed to do

> **Supersedes two earlier versions.** The first made his dark history sexual
> misconduct, which put his sexuality on the board as a lever. The second made
> him a child soldier who took part in an atrocity — which required a grisly
> deed, forced him to have been very young, and pushed the event decades back.
>
> **Neither is needed.** His wound is **an omission**, and it is the better
> version on every axis.

**He knew something terrible was going to happen, and he did not act.** Not as a
child — as **an adult**, with standing, in a position to be heard. Recently
enough that it is still the most present fact in his life.

A bounded, local atrocity in a community he served: days, one town, people he
could name. He saw it building. He understood exactly what he was looking at.
**He said nothing, or said it too late, or said it so carefully that it
achieved nothing.**

##### Why this is better in four ways

| | the old version | this |
|---|---|---|
| requires | a child committing atrocity | **a man not making a phone call** |
| his age then | very young, decades ago | **adult, recent — it can be five years** |
| the liability | a supernatural reading laid over a named real genocide | **a small, local, unnamed event — no real atrocity is being explained** |
| the rhyme with play | a previous instance of what the player *does* | **a previous instance of [the player's actual main verb](MECHANICS.md#the-dark-game-is-mostly-suppression-not-incitement)** |

The fourth is the one that matters. The design's real verb turned out to be
**making good people fail to intervene.** His entire life is organised around
one instance of that, from the inside.

##### The theory is now exactly the right size

**It is never established whether a parasite was involved.** The possibility is
raised and stays a theory — but the theory has changed shape, and improved:

> It is no longer *"did something make me commit an atrocity"*, which needed a
> parasite capable of enormous things. It is **"did something make it hard for
> me to speak"** — which is the smallest, cheapest, most ordinary thing a
> parasite can do, and therefore entirely plausible.

[Suppression is the cheap
operation](MECHANICS.md#and-it-is-cheap-which-is-the-whole-problem). The theory
no longer strains the mechanics; it sits precisely on top of them.

And the mundane explanation remains complete: fear, careerism, deference,
uncertainty, the reasonable-sounding plan to raise it properly next week.
**Nothing requires explaining**, which is why the theory is about his need for
it rather than about the events.

##### The exoneration he can never have

If something suppressed him, he is absolved. If not, he chose silence. **He
cannot find out.**

> **A possible innocence permanently out of reach** — heavier than either guilt
> or innocence, and now attached to the most common moral failure there is
> rather than to a rare and monstrous one.

**And the player is in the same position with more information.** You know these
things exist and exactly how cheaply they do this one, and *it still does not
resolve.*

##### It gives the old priest's sermons a referent

The [old priest](#the-old-priest) preaches about *demons that drive people to
unspeakable deeds en masse*, and the young one has stood in a town where that
happened and watched himself do nothing about it.

**He does not disagree with the old man. He cannot afford to agree with him
either.** Which is a much better reason for their strained courtesy than
doctrine, and neither will ever say it.

##### [proposal] His symbiote dates from it

New symbiotes come from failed parasites. His is new. **If the event is recent,
a parasite failed somewhere near him at about that time.**

That is an inference the player can assemble and nobody confirms — and it is
poised exactly where the design wants its evidence: it makes his theory *more*
plausible without making it true. A parasite was there. It does not follow that
it was in him, or that it reached him, or that it mattered.

> He will never have that inference. **The player gets the one piece of evidence
> he needed and cannot deliver it** — until, perhaps, they can, which is what
> [brokerage](MECHANICS.md#4-information-brokerage--between-humans) is for, and
> which would be either the kindest or the cruellest thing in the game.

**Its profile is unchanged:** born in catastrophe with nothing to calibrate on,
tuned to him alone ever since. **Deep, not wide** — maximum individual
resistance, zero network integration, no homing. Years rather than decades,
which makes it newer and stranger still.

##### Scope: the mechanics now supply the limit for free

The earlier version needed an argument to keep the theory small — a larval
parasite cannot cause a genocide, the leash is tight, the window is weeks. That
argument was doing real work and it was always slightly strained.

**The omission version needs none of it.** One man, one hesitation, one week.
That is inside the leash with room to spare, and it keeps the scale honest
without the design having to insist.

> The theory was never allowed to be *"an alien caused that town's worst
> week."* It is now **"was something in the room when I decided to wait"** —
> which is small enough to be true and small enough not to matter, and he will
> never know which.

##### His symbiote is new, and adrift

**Resolved in favour of the event-born reading.** A parasite present at that
time would have failed and left **a new** symbiote — and the reasoning that
settles it is mechanical:

> **An old symbiote would have steered him back.** They are calibrated to a
> community and nudge toward the conditions their models describe, so a
> displaced one self-corrects (see [An old symbiote steers toward
> home](ECOSYSTEM.md#an-old-symbiote-steers-toward-home)). You never find one far
> from where it was made.

**A new one has no set point.** It was born with no community calibration and
nothing to steer toward. He was set adrift and never found his way back.

It also **proves an older symbiote community exists where he comes from** — a
parasite can only operate where symbiotes already are, so there must have been
an ancient population there. Which is inference the player can make, and nobody
confirms.

**Its profile:** born in catastrophe with nothing else to calibrate on, it has
been tuned to him alone ever since. **Deep, not wide** — maximum individual
resistance, zero network integration, no homing. Years rather than decades, now
that the event is recent, which makes it newer and stranger still.

##### His perceptiveness is his own, and it indicts him

Dropping the ancient lineage removes the driver-library explanation for his
uncanny read on people. **The replacement needs no mechanism at all — he is
simply perceptive, and always was.**

> Which is far worse for him than a gift forged by trauma. **He saw it clearly.
> That is what his perceptiveness was for, the one time it counted, and he used
> it to understand in precise detail exactly what he was declining to prevent.**

He cannot tell himself he did not know. Reading people is the thing he is best
at in the world.

Which splits the familiarity/otherness cleanly:

- **familiarity** — *cultural.* There was an older symbiote community where he is
  from, with its own traditions, and the island's folklore rhymes with stories
  he already knows
- **otherness** — *mechanical.* His symbiote receives island signal it cannot
  resolve, a constant low wrongness underneath everything

##### Do not name the place

**[recommendation]** Keep the register — a parish, a town, a week that went
wrong. **Leave the place and the conflict unspecified.**

This mattered urgently in the child-soldier version, where a supernatural
footnote risked attaching to a named genocide with living survivors. **The
omission version has largely defused that**, because the event is small, local
and unremarkable — the kind of thing that happens in many places and is named in
none of them.

The recommendation stands anyway. Unnamed costs nothing: the horror is identical
and the reader supplies the rest. And it keeps the eclipse constraint useful
rather than awkward — the moment a real event is named, somebody checks whether
there was an eclipse, and **either answer is bad.**

## The arc

The entity's development is the spine of the game, and it runs in three broad
movements:

1. **Instinct.** You cannot read humans, only push them. You act on whatever is
   in front of you. Your reach is tiny.
2. **Pattern.** You learn individual people — their emotional registers, what
   moves them. You begin to steer rather than shove. Your reach extends in both
   space and time.
3. **Contact.** Humans start to perceive the possession. Manipulation alone
   stops working; you have to actually interact with them, and they can act
   against you.

Movements 2 and 3 have a single job: **engineering a mass emotional event** (see
[The transcendence event](MECHANICS.md#the-transcendence-event)). The entity pursues it as a
compulsion it cannot explain, and the player rehearses it across timelines until
one configuration holds.

The arc is not only narrative — it is what the two resources demand. You begin
by farming the edges of the community and end by having to reach its middle,
which is exactly where the resistance lives.

## How the player learns anything

The design wants the entity ignorant and the interface opaque, but the player
still needs information — most urgently that a direct link is the difference
between symbiosis and extinction. The resolution is to route all of it through
**unreliable in-world interpreters.**

### Humans are the exposition channel

Because the entity thinks with borrowed cognition, **inhabiting someone who is
investigating the anomaly means their reasoning becomes your reasoning.** They
theorise about the thing in the crater; you are thinking about yourself. The
entity acquires self-knowledge only by riding someone studying it.

> **Knowledge and threat are the same curve.** The humans closing in on what you
> are *is* your information channel. You cannot become informed without becoming
> hunted.

This is the tightest structural loop in the design. It also gives the third act
its shape: the scenes where you learn most are the scenes where you are most
nearly caught.

### What each discipline can discover

The exposition channels are not interchangeable. Each can reach a different part
of the truth, and **none of them can reach all of it.**

#### The biologist — who can detect *you*

- **The epidemiological signature.** Spread happens by skin contact along the
  intimacy graph, which produces a contact network shaped exactly like an
  infectious outbreak. Contact tracing is a century old. Someone doing field
  epidemiology in a town where people have suddenly started touching, fighting
  and falling into bed more than usual maps your movements precisely, with no
  idea what they are mapping.

  > **This is not lore. It is the player's own trail, and it scales with play.**
  > A careful run leaves a weak signal; an aggressive one leaves an outbreak
  > with a legible index case.

- **The endosymbiont.** The mitochondrial analogy is literal enough to be
  *found*. Mitochondria were visible under microscopes for decades before anyone
  understood them; endosymbiotic theory only arrived in 1967. So the symbiote is
  a structure present in nearly all human tissue — catalogued, assumed to be a
  staining artifact or a variant of something known. **Visible for a century, in
  the literature, unremarked.**

- **Which reveals the unclaimed.** Enough tissue surveys and some people turn
  out to *lack* it. Flagged as an anomaly in the data long before anyone can say
  what it means — and the player can learn who they are by riding the biologist
  who found them. **The lifeboat is identified by the science that is hunting
  you.**

#### The astronomer — who can detect your predecessors

Eclipse paths correlated against the localities of an unclassifiable fossil
taxon (see [The husk is in the fossil
record](ECOSYSTEM.md#the-husk-is-in-the-fossil-record)). Deep history, not the present.

#### The counter-play: prevent a conversation

**Neither reaches the truth alone.** A fossil correlation is a curiosity; an
unknown organelle and a contact map are a curiosity. Together they are the
answer.

> So the third act hands the player a very specific objective: **keep those two
> people from comparing notes.** Not murder, not evasion — prevent a
> conversation. In a small town where everyone eats in the same room that is
> genuinely hard, and it means spending your remaining shell and credit on
> stopping two people from becoming friends.

### Two wrong models, no adjudication

| | gives you | wrong about |
|---|---|---|
| **the scientists** | a mechanistic account — testable, partial | everything the instruments cannot reach |
| **a mystic** | a metaphorical account — resonant, partial | everything the metaphor flattens |

**Neither is authoritative and the game never settles it.** Nothing ever
explains the cosmology; characters explain it incorrectly in two directions and
the player synthesises. That preserves ignorance as atmosphere while still
delivering what the player needs, and it means the design can hold facts (the
shell, the species, the three outcomes) that no line of dialogue is ever
responsible for stating correctly.

### The folklore is a real record

Every symbiote here is an expired parasite, so **parasites have come to this
place before — at previous eclipses.** The community's stories about possession
have a genuine referent, old and garbled, and they are attached to exactly the
event now approaching.

That is why the cults exist, why the folklore has observances, and why the
scientific interest in local historical anomalies is not idle.

The mystic may therefore be closer to right than the scientist on specific
points — not because mysticism works, but because the stories are old and were
once observations. That is a good reason for an island community to have
exactly the right superstitions, and it is free.

**[The islanders' tradition is the best-preserved copy](#their-myths-are-the-most-accurate-account-on-the-island)**
— correct about symbiote inheritance, correct about what arrives at totality,
wrong only in attributing the eclipse to the visitor. And its observance is
[actionable in the strongest possible sense](#the-ceremony-is-an-invitation):
it is the island's best transcendence event, and it was built to host one.

- **[open]** How much the *other* traditions retained. The cult is the same
  observation inherited through the church; the question is whether it kept a
  practice or only a secret.

## Reference points

- **The Forgotten City**, **Outer Wilds** — knowledge as the only real

### For the register

The design's machinery — possession, escalating non-consensual influence, a cult,
a ritual fixed to an astronomical date — is the machinery of exploitation horror.
These are the works that use the same parts seriously:

- **The Wicker Man** (1973) — a remote community, an outsider, a fixed ritual
  date, a cosmology with its own internal logic. The direct structural ancestor.
- **Midsommar** — collective ecstatic ritual including sexual rite, filmed
  anthropologically rather than for titillation. The clearest demonstration that
  the difference is authorial stance, not content.
- **Under the Skin** — a non-human predator on humans, borrowed and incomplete
  interiority, never explained. The closest reference for the POV problem.
- **Picnic at Hanging Rock** — awe, disappearance, a refusal to account for
  itself.
- **Annihilation** — biology that does not care about you and is not malicious.
- **"Goliath"**, Neil Gaiman (1999) — an official Matrix tie-in using
  humans-as-computation rather than batteries. The closest existing execution of
  this premise. *(Note: the widespread claim that this was the Matrix's original
  script concept appears apocryphal — the earliest known drafts still have
  batteries. Gaiman's story is probably the source of the belief.)*

> **The distinction worth holding onto:** in the Matrix, either version, humans
> are unconscious, podded and fully simulated — the horror is imprisonment. Here
> nobody is imprisoned. People are awake, free, living their own lives, and
> something borrows their spare capacity while they do. **That nightmare
> requires a prison; this one requires only a Tuesday.**
