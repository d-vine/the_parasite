## The species

### What kind of being this is

**Neither species has consciousness or an ego in the human sense.**

- **Intent and agency, yes. Interiority of its own, no.** The parasite wants
  things and acts on the world without having an inner life to do it from.
- **Its being is mediated through its hosts.** It experiences life *as* the
  human it inhabits. **It only has a self in relation to its hosts** — the self
  is not a fixed thing it carries around but something that exists in the having
  of hosts.
- **It co-opts human cognition to reason** — not because it is conscious, but
  because borrowed thinking is useful for operating a borrowed body. **Reasoning
  is a co-opted limb, not an inner life.** It uses thought the way it uses
  hands, and acquiring more of it never produces an interior.
- **It runs on the social brain.** Most of a human brain operates the body;
  most of the remainder simulates other people in order to predict them. That
  module is exactly what the parasite needs, and one brain's worth of it is
  enough to run on.

> **This bounds the toolkit.** The entity's powers can only ever be *social* —
> observe, predict, nudge — because that is what the borrowed hardware does. It
> cannot do arithmetic or move objects, because the module does not.
- **Transcendence is metamorphosis, not ambition.** An adult merging
  universe-spanning timelines is performing *biology*, not a willed act, however
  unimaginable the computation involved. The caterpillar is not pursuing a goal.
- **Adults are processes running on the fabric of reality** rather than
  creatures inhabiting it. (Possibly everything is. The game need not say.)

Consequences:

- **The entity has instincts and faculties, not purposes.** It does not want
  things in the way a character wants things.
- **It has faculties, not concepts**, which is why the interface presents
  incomprehensible labels that reveal themselves only through use.
- **The entity has no visible interior, ever.** Not a diminished one, not one
  that grows — none. It [runs no self-model](#why-the-entity-has-no-interior),
  and [the player supplies one](#the-player-supplies-the-interior).
- **Both endings lose the self, in opposite directions.** Transcendence leaves
  an adult with no hosts at all: unbounded, and nobody home. Symbiosis contracts
  the self to a single host, permanently — which is to say, near enough, to a
  person.

#### Three parties, and one of them inherits the equipment

**The central fact, and most of the ecology follows from it.**

| | what it is |
|---|---|
| **the parasite** | an organism of quantum-encoded information. **The shell is its body** |
| **the shell** | **its organ** — storage, and the branch-sampling faculty. Not a creature, and it has no reproduction of its own because body parts do not need one |
| **the limb** | **a separate organism.** Tissue-dwelling, with its own life cycle and its own reproduction, recruited as an actuator |
| **the host** | the animal the limb lives in, and the community it spreads through |

##### What a symbiote actually is

At transcendence or expiry the parasite goes, and **the shell is left behind,
still working, with the operator gone.** The limb is standing right there,
tethered to it.

> **A limb with a shell can see forward and remember. A limb without one cannot.**

So the recruited actuator inherits a faculty it could never have possessed, and
begins using it for its own ends — which are simply the ends a tissue-dwelling
organism has: stay alive, stay in a viable habitat, keep spreading.

> **The guardian is the servant left holding the master's instrument.**

##### Which is where the opposition comes from, and it needs no regulation

The party doing the resisting is the party with a reproductive future:

| | stake in the community |
|---|---|
| **the spent shell** | **none.** It is an object |
| **the limb** | **everything.** The host community is its habitat *and* its route of dispersal |

So resistance is not a post-reproductive conspecific picking a pointless fight,
and it is not homeostasis. It is **an organism defending the thing it lives in
and spreads through**, from something that will spend it and leave.

> **They do not have competing claims. They have incompatible discount rates on
> the same resource.** The arriving parasite has until totality and will spend
> everything; the limb has an indefinite horizon and needs the substrate intact.
>
> The resident's position is not *this is mine*. It is **this one is going to
> burn the house down and then move out.**

And the irony the design already runs on gets sharper: [the limb is the only
route in](MECHANICS.md#they-are-the-bridge) **and** the party with the most to
lose. You have to recruit the one creature that is correct to refuse you.

> **Why the shell has no reproduction route.** Because it is not an organism. An
> adult makes new ones when it seeds, and the chrysalis image was saying so all
> along: **the shell is the body that gets left.**

##### What they are made of

**Working assumption, stated once and never argued in-game: consciousness is
something a body does.** A process the body runs, not a substance it has.

On that assumption, nothing here is supernatural and all of it is the same kind
of thing:

| | what it is made of | executes on | anchored to |
|---|---|---|---|
| **a human consciousness** | the body it is a process of | that body | — |
| **the parasite** | quantum-encoded information | a host's brain, through a recruited limb | **the shell — its own body** |
| **a limb, while ridden** | a population of **physical units** in the host's tissue | a host's brain | the shell, loosely |
| **a limb, after the rider goes** | the same population, now [holding the shell itself](#the-eclipse-is-what-creates-the-tether) | a host's brain | **the shell it inherited** |
| **an adult** | itself | the fabric of spacetime | nothing |

**A limb is material, not information.** It is a population of units living in
the host — replicating there, built on the mitochondrial pattern: a minimal
inherited core, with every piece of apparatus assembled out of host proteins. Once
established it **runs the way consciousness runs**, on the same tissue, by the
same kind of process.

**But it reproduces as information.** The units themselves never leave the body
they grew in; what crosses to a new host is a targeted package of instructions
for building a fresh population there — see [how a limb
reproduces](#how-a-limb-reproduces).

What changes at transcendence is not the substrate. It is **the tether.**

##### Which makes a human a holobiont, with one more resident

The organism is not an intrusion into an otherwise unified body. **It is one more
passenger in a body that was never one thing.**

| | |
|---|---|
| bacterial to human cells | roughly **1:1** — ~3.8 × 10¹³ against ~3.0 × 10¹³ |
| mitochondria | their own genome of 37 genes; the other ~1,500 proteins supplied by the host |
| the human genome | **~8% endogenous retrovirus** — and **syncytin**, which builds the placenta, is a captured viral envelope gene |
| gut flora | vitamin K, immune training, and mood through a gut–brain axis |

> **Nobody experiences their mitochondria as a passenger.** Which is why nothing
> in this setting reads as possession, and why [carrying a symbiote is
> indistinguishable from ordinary
> intuition](MECHANICS.md#which-makes-a-symbiote-a-sixth-sense).

And it makes the species split biological rather than moral:

| | |
|---|---|
| **the limb** | **flora.** A separate organism — settled, chronic, inherited, participating, adapted to the niche |
| **the parasite** | **a pathogen.** Acute, foreign, uncalibrated, newly arrived, and **of a different lineage entirely** |

*Symbiotes are an immune system* stops being analogy. They do not resist because
they object — they resist the way a microbiome resists a colonist, by already
holding the niche and being fitted to it when the arrival is not.

> **What a discovery would actually be.** Not aliens: **an undescribed
> endosymbiont in an isolated human population, with heritable behavioural
> effects.** A paper and a career, which is what a small station with a
> collections policy might plausibly stumble into.
>
> Each party holds half. The islanders have tracked the organism accurately for a
> thousand years with no framework. Biology has the framework — holobiont,
> endosymbiosis, Margulis — and has never looked.

##### Folding multiplies the limbs

[A run that folds](MECHANICS.md#timelines) produces variant versions of the same
limb across timelines. **Only the one that transcends leaves.** Every other
variant remains attached to the shell.

> So **one shell can anchor many symbiotes** — all of them versions of the same
> limb, all of them tethered to the same body. A shell with a lot of guardians
> around it is a shell whose limb folded a great many times.

##### Which means a symbiote is not cut loose. It is attached to a dead body.

The shell's reserve is spent at the eclipse. The body persists — it does not
degrade — but there is no power left in it for anything a shell does.

| | before the eclipse | after |
|---|---|---|
| **tether** | yes | **yes, unchanged** |
| **reserve** | finite, spendable | **spent** |
| **can create timelines** | yes | no |
| **can overpower a symbiote** | yes | no |

**[The leash](MECHANICS.md#the-leash) therefore still applies.** A symbiote
cannot go far from its shell, which is why symbiotes are local, why [an old one
steers toward home](#an-old-symbiote-steers-toward-home), and why one is never
found far from where it was made.

> **Home is where its body is.** The community-calibration account is what that
> looks like from outside.

##### Why the entity has no interior

Not because it is a different order of being. **Because it does not run the
self-modelling part.**

> A human consciousness is the body modelling itself. The parasite [runs on the
> social brain](#what-kind-of-being-this-is) — the machinery that simulates
> *other* people — and nothing else.
>
> **It is an other-model with no self-model attached.** All the apparatus for
> reading minds; none for having one.

##### The parasite has a body and the symbiote does not

This single asymmetry accounts for most of the difference between them.

| | because it has a shell | because it has nothing |
|---|---|---|
| **host death** | survives; the storage is elsewhere | **ends**, unless it has already [built a driver into a relative](#how-symbiotes-persist-between-eclipses) and can transfer |
| **timelines** | the shell is the organ that opens them, and [discards all but one at a fold](MECHANICS.md#what-a-fold-actually-consumes) | cannot open one, only inhabit |
| **memory** | the drivers are *in* the shell — not a metaphor, the only storage there is | holds only what its host's tissue holds |
| **reserve** | finite capacity of its own, in the shell — [storage, not energy](#the-mechanism) | **none at all.** Every cycle it uses is one the host is not using |
| **reproduction** | can reach adulthood | cannot; the population is [conserved](#how-symbiotes-persist-between-eclipses) |

##### Which restates several rules as consequences

- **[Minimal interference](#minimal-interference)** is not restraint. A symbiote
  runs on borrowed hardware with no reserve to fall back on. **It cannot afford
  to do much.**
- **[Losing the shell is the whole of the failure
  state](#the-shell).** You do not lose your mind, you lose **your body**, and
  become a process with no substrate of its own, dependent on one person.
- **[Communion](story/islanders.md#the-practice-is-technique-not-spirituality) crosses no
  gap.** The guardian is a process running on the shaman's own tissue; the
  altered state makes local data legible to the self-model. She is not reaching
  outside herself.
- **Eviction terminates a process with nowhere to go.** With no substrate of its
  own, a displaced symbiote transfers or stops. **[open]** which.
- **[The shell-retaining symbiote](#proposal-the-shell-retaining-symbiote)** is
  precisely a symbiote that kept its body, which is why it has never existed.

##### What this does not claim

The game never argues the assumption. It is a frame for keeping the three kinds
of thing consistent with one another, and it has one visible consequence:
**nothing here is supernatural, and the entity is not categorically unlike the
people it rides** — only differently stored, and missing a self.

#### The player supplies the interior

**The entity is never given an inner life by the text. The player lends it one,
through what they make it do.**

This is a hard rule, not a tendency. The viewpoint slot is empty, and nothing in
the game fills it.

##### What the prose may never do

| never | because |
|---|---|
| **a verb of internal state for the entity** — *you feel, you want, something in you stirs, it is drawn to* | there is nothing there to be in that state |
| **report satisfaction, dread, relief, curiosity or reluctance** | all of those are the player's, and the text must not pre-empt them |
| **narrate the entity from outside** | no vantage exists on it, and no vocabulary that is not borrowed |
| **motivate a choice in its choice text** | the option is an action, never an inclination. *Press on his fear*, not *press on his fear because you resent him* |

##### What the prose does instead

**The host's interior is fully available, and should be rich.** The narration is
perception: what the host senses, what the host feels, what can be read off the
people in the room.

> **You are reading somebody else's interior, continuously, and never your own,
> because there is not one.** Host choice is therefore a narrative variable — a
> researcher's inner life is a different text from a grieving woman's — but what
> changes is **whose interior is on the page**, not the flavour of the entity's.

##### The tropism is an affordance, not a feeling

The entity's pull toward gathering people cannot be *felt*. It appears as **what
the interface offers**: some options exist, others do not, and the pattern of
what is available is the only trace of its nature.

> **Its nature shows up as what is possible, never as what is wanted.**

##### Where the ambiguity now lives

The old formulation had the entity unable to distinguish its wanting from its
host's. With no entity interior, that moves: **the player may not be able to
tell whether an impulse they are acting on came from the host's state they were
reading, or from themselves.** Free, permanent and mechanically live, as before.

### The parasite (you)

You hatch from a seed in a **larval** state. **A larva has its own power and its
own memory and no mind at all** — it borrows every thought it has from a host.
The purpose of the larval stage is to reach enough borrowed processing, at one
moment, to **transcend** into the adult form — possibly the final form.

> **It does not feed on feeling.** There is only cognition. **Emotion is the
> condition under which a mind becomes usable**, not a substance the entity
> consumes — see [the
> valve](MECHANICS.md#compute-is-bandwidth-and-emotion-is-the-valve).

An adult perceives, harvests and manipulates the **quantum fields that
constitute reality** directly. It no longer needs hosts. It stretches across
vast regions of spacetime.

**Transcendence happens in one timeline — the one where you connected enough
minds. That timeline is the only one that survives. The rest collapse.**

> This does not contradict "previous timelines keep existing." They persist for
> the whole of the larval stage. Transcendence is the event that ends that
> condition.

**Reproduction** is the merging of two distinct timelines: the things each adult
influenced are combined into one, and the other side of the overlap is
discarded. This works best when the two zones of influence are far apart, so
adults scatter their seeds widely across space and time. It is why you are here,
and why you are alone.

> [Folding](MECHANICS.md#timelines) is a small, solitary version of the same
> operation: one larva merging its own timelines because it has no partner to
> merge with.

> **[open]** Merging is forbidden by the physics as recorded — see [the note
> under folding](MECHANICS.md#folding-is-reproduction-performed-by-something-that-cannot-yet-reproduce).

#### The shell

**It is not equipment. It is the body** — see [the shell is the
organism](#three-parties-and-one-of-them-inherits-the-equipment). The seed is a
shell with a limb folded inside it.

It holds the entire memory, it is what opens timelines and overpowers a
symbiote's resistance, and it is what binds you, since you can never leave its
reach. The power supply is the host's — see [The
mechanism](#the-mechanism).

> **The thinking is somebody else's. The body is yours.** Power, storage and
> anchor here; processing out there, on loan.

**The reserve is finite and never replenished.** If it is gone before you
transcend you do not die and you are not cut loose — **you stay attached to a
body that can no longer do anything.** That is the failure state, and it is the
state every symbiote in this world is already in.

It is also not annihilation. Since the self is borrowed from hosts, a tethered
limb keeps exactly as much of one as a single permanent host can lend it: one
life, one perspective, no capacity to rewrite anything. Near enough to being a
person.

Mechanics under [The shell](MECHANICS.md#the-shell).

### The symbiote

**Local, and dependent on humans.** A symbiote perceives and inhabits the
timelines a parasite creates, but **cannot create one itself**. Its goal is the
survival and thriving of its humans, and therefore the advance of a timeline
that is good for them.

Each symbiote inhabits a particular person, and they differ by age:

| | host | emotional register | reach |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Young** | peripheral community members — teenagers and the like | volatile, high amplitude | one person |
| **Old** | important members of the community | stable | sometimes several people at once |

The older ones hold multiple hosts — the same faculty you acquire late. They are
further along a road you are on.

#### How a limb reproduces

[The limb is a separate
organism](#three-parties-and-one-of-them-inherits-the-equipment), so it has a
life cycle of its own, and it is the only one of the three parties that does.
The shell does not reproduce, because an organ has no reproductive route. The
parasite reproduces only by transcending. **The limb reproduces the ordinary
way: it transmits information, and the receiver builds the copy.**

##### What crosses, and what does not

| | |
|---|---|
| **what crosses** | **a package of DNA and a handful of proteins** — viral in form and in delivery, and targeted in a way no virus is |
| **what it does on arrival** | sets up production of limb units **out of host tissue**, the same way the units in the issuer were built |
| **what does not cross** | **cells.** Nothing of the issuer's own body makes the trip |
| **what is not touched** | **the host genome.** The package is not written into the germ line, and children do not inherit it genetically. It is **carried, not encoded** |

Which keeps the limb exactly where [the holobiont
table](#which-makes-a-human-a-holobiont-with-one-more-resident) already put it:
**flora, not a gene.** You acquire your microbiome from your mother. You do not
inherit it.

> **It is the mitochondrial pattern again, one step earlier.** A minimal foreign
> core with every piece of apparatus assembled out of host proteins — and now
> the core itself arrives as *instructions* rather than as an object.

A real form for the delivery, if one is ever wanted: **~8% of the human genome is
endogenous retrovirus**, and syncytin, which builds the placenta, is a captured
viral envelope gene. Getting a working gene package into human tissue with
tissue-specific targeting is a thing viruses do and a thing clinical vectors are
built to do.

*(What this deliberately is **not**: a transmissible cell line. Canine
transmissible venereal tumour and devil facial tumour disease are real,
ancient, and spread as living cells from the original animal. Here nothing of
the donor survives in the recipient — which is why a limb has no single point of
origin to trace.)*

##### The brake is targeting, and targeting is tolerance

A package written for one person does not work in another. That is why this is
not an epidemic, and the reason is the most ordinary one in human biology: **a
body destroys what it does not recognise.**

So the package has to be written *for* the recipient, which requires the issuer
to read that specific body first, in detail, over a long time.

| route | why it works | cost |
|---|---|---|
| **kin** | **shared haplotypes.** A parent and child share one HLA haplotype obligately; full siblings are identical by descent across both roughly a quarter of the time. Most of the targeting is already done before anyone starts | cheap, and comparatively fast |
| **a long sexual relationship** | repeated exposure to one partner's antigens **builds partner-specific tolerance.** This is real reproductive immunology: short sexual cohabitation before conception is a documented preeclampsia risk factor, and prolonged exposure to the same partner is protective | expensive, and slow — years |
| **anything briefer** | nothing. Not a handshake, not a field season, not one affair | — |

> **Prolonged, intense, and with one particular person** is not a decorative
> condition. It is the whole brake. The two human arrangements that satisfy it
> are **raising a child** and **a long relationship with one partner**, and
> there is no third.

##### Which runs on a channel already in the design

Reading a body takes contact and bandwidth, and there is already a published
physical layer for it: [jumping is a file
transfer](MECHANICS.md#jumping-is-a-file-transfer) — Human Body Communication,
IEEE 802.15.6, in which **contact duration × skin area is the channel.**

Which collapses two slow operations into one:

> **Building the driver and writing the seed are the same job.** Both are the
> issuer learning one specific body well enough to address it. A limb that has
> spent fifteen years learning a daughter well enough to relocate into her has,
> by that fact, spent fifteen years learning her well enough to seed her.

##### A limb is not a symbiote

Which is the consequence that has to be stated plainly, because the limb now
reproduces and the older rule said nothing did.

| | reproduces? | how many |
|---|---|---|
| **the limb** | **yes** — targeted transfer into kin and long partners | uncommon, concentrated in lineages, quietly persistent wherever families are close |
| **the shell** | **no.** It is an organ | **conserved.** One shell is one shell, and the only source is a clutch |

**A symbiote is a limb holding a shell.** The limb is the common part; the shell
is the scarce part.

- **A spreading limb does not make symbiotes.** A limb with no shell is flora —
  chronic, acquired by contact, participating in the body, and with **no
  foresight whatsoever**: nothing to sample timelines with, nothing to steer by
- **Humanity still has no gentle guiding hand.** The number of symbiotes in the
  world is the number of shells left behind by failed transcendences, decaying.
  It does not grow between eclipses
- **The shell inherits into a limb, not into a person.** When a holder dies the
  shell goes to someone who **already carries a limb** — which is why it goes to
  family, and why it can fail

> **The limb population is the infrastructure the shell lives on.** A lineage
> that keeps producing carriers keeps its shell. A lineage that stops strands
> one — and a stranded shell is not a symbiote looking for a home. It is an
> object on a shelf.

##### Seeding from a shell requires a driver

One asymmetry, and it carries weight: **a shell can seed a limb into a host only
while a parasite is driving it.** A free shell cannot start a lineage.

| issuer | can seed a new host? | |
|---|---|---|
| **a shell under a parasite** | **yes** | this is how a lineage *starts*, and it happens only around an eclipse |
| **a free shell** | **no** | an organ with nobody holding it decides nothing |
| **a limb** | **yes**, into kin and long partners | this is how a lineage *persists* |

So there are two populations with two different sources, and the slow common one
is what the rare one needs in order to survive at all.

#### What a limb does to its host: it makes them want to touch shells

**Unfailingly, in every carrier, with no exceptions.** A limb produces a
standing attraction to shells and an impulse to put a hand on one. It is the one
behavioural signature the organism has, and it is the only thing a limb does
that a host could in principle notice.

##### The evolutionary logic is short

A limb cannot acquire a shell. **Its host can.**

| | |
|---|---|
| a limb alone | flora. No foresight, no sampling, no faculty of any kind |
| a limb holding a shell | a symbiote |
| the distance between them | **one act of physical contact** |

So the limb does the only thing available to it: it makes the host **go and find
one**, and reach out. Everything the organism could ever become is on the far
side of a touch, and the limb is not the thing with hands.

> **It is not loyalty and it is not service.** The limb is upgrading itself, and
> the host is the mechanism.

##### Host behavioural manipulation is ordinary, documented parasitology

Nothing here needs inventing. The literature is old and unglamorous:

| | |
|---|---|
| ***Dicrocoelium dendriticum*** | the lancet fluke drives an ant to climb a grass blade at dusk and **clamp its mandibles shut on the tip**, so a grazing sheep eats it. Worked out in the early 1950s |
| ***Leucochloridium paradoxum*** | pulsating broodsacs fill a snail's eyestalks and drive it into the open, where birds take it. Described in **1835** |
| ***Ophiocordyceps*** | the ant's death-grip bite, at a species-specific height and humidity |
| hairworms | drive terrestrial insects to enter water and drown |
| ***Sacculina*** | castrates a crab and makes it tend the parasite's brood as if it were its own eggs |
| ***Toxoplasma gondii*** | reduces infected rodents' aversion to cat odour |

The mechanism is correspondingly ordinary — secreted compounds acting on an
existing reward or aversion circuit. **A limb has a far better instrument than a
fluke does**: it is already a population of units in the host's tissue, built
out of host proteins, sitting where neuromodulation happens.

##### What it feels like, which is the point

Not a compulsion. **A preference**, and a deniable one.

| | |
|---|---|
| what the host experiences | finding them beautiful. Wanting to hold one. Going back to the shelf. Picking it up while thinking about something else |
| what the host never experiences | being made to |
| what a host says if asked | that they have always liked them — which is true, and is the whole of what they know |

> **It is indistinguishable from taste.** Which is why it has never once been
> reported as a symptom, by anyone, in a thousand years.

*(Distinct from [the parasite's tropism](#the-tropism-is-an-affordance-not-a-feeling),
which is not felt at all and shows up only as what the interface offers. This one
is felt, by a human, as liking something.)*

##### At population scale it is a collection, and then a religion

One carrier with a shell-impulse is a person with an odd fondness. **A kin group
of carriers over forty generations is a culture organised around shells** — and
[the island's religion](story/islanders.md#the-shells-were-the-religion) stops needing a
separate explanation for why *these* people, on *this* rock, built everything
around *that* object.

It also explains the collection in the house without a word of mysticism. [The
developer spent decades acquiring shells](story/developers.md#what-he-was-actually-doing) by
purchase, swindle, bribery and threat, with a collector's cover story that was
true as far as it went. **Collectors collect. Carriers collect shells.**

> **[liability] The tropism supplies the impulse, not the content.** It must
> never be allowed to read as *the islanders were drugged into a religion*.
> Everything they know about shells they learned the way anyone learns anything
> — by watching, for a very long time, and being right. The tropism only
> explains **why they were the ones doing the watching.** A thousand years of
> accurate observation is still a thousand years of accurate observation.

> **[open]** Whether the tropism has range, or only works on sight and
> proximity. The conservative reading: it is an impulse to *touch*, and it
> explains why [the finder](STORY.md#she-was-waiting-for-it) went toward the fall
> instead of away, and why she put her hand on it instead of reporting it — not
> how she knew where to go. The strong reading gives carriers a bearing, which
> is a much bigger mechanism and should be bought deliberately if at all.

#### How symbiotes persist between eclipses

Totality recurs at a point every ~375 years *on average* — far longer than a
human life — so symbiotes must outlive their hosts for the attractor to work.

> **The interval is softer than the average suggests.** That figure is a
> long-run mean, not a period; the real distribution is wildly irregular.
> Carbondale, Illinois sat under totality in 2017 and again in 2024. **This
> island is in a cluster** — a rare run of totalities across a few centuries —
> so the gap to bridge is decades to a century, not four centuries every time.

##### The shell relocates on host death. It does not multiply

**Ruled out:** hosts travelling to seek eclipses. It would make transcendence
events common and drain the wonder from the one the game is about.

**The mechanism is already in the design** (see [Positioning across
events](MECHANICS.md#3-positioning-across-events), which turns on forcing a symbiote onto a
weaker host by killing its current one):

> A symbiote persists with its host for life and **relocates when the host
> dies**, into someone it has spent years building a driver for. Usually family.

What moves is **the shell**, into a relative who already carries a limb — see [a
limb is not a symbiote](#a-limb-is-not-a-symbiote). The limb in the dying host
dies with the host; the limb in the receiving host was seeded years earlier and
is already in place.

- **No multiplication, so no saturation.** The shell population is conserved,
  not growing. Most humans have never held one — **humanity has no gentle
  guiding hand.**
- **The age hierarchy survives.** Old symbiotes stay on old, important people
  precisely because they do not jump early. If they moved to children on
  schedule, the elderly would be unclaimed and the hierarchy would invert.

##### Why family, and the mechanism is the same one

Both halves of the transfer favour kin, for the same reason and independently:

| | what family buys it |
|---|---|
| **the limb**, seeding a new carrier | **shared haplotypes.** Most of the targeting work is done by descent — see [targeting is tolerance](#the-brake-is-targeting-and-targeting-is-tolerance) |
| **the shell**, relocating on death | **a driver that is an edit, not a rewrite**, and a carrier already in place |

Neither is a host-jump. Nothing travels as a body. The conditions the older
argument named turn out to be the literal transfer mechanism for both:

- **similarity** — a relative's driver is a short edit, not a rewrite
- **duration** — years of proximity is what slow driver-building needs
- **bandwidth** — contact duration × skin area *is* the channel (see [Jumping is
  a file transfer](MECHANICS.md#jumping-is-a-file-transfer)), and **nothing in human life
  has more of both than raising a child.** Parent–infant contact is the
  highest-bandwidth link humans have.

*(And it runs both ways. **Microchimerism** is real and well documented:
fetal cells persist in the mother for decades after a birth, and maternal cells
persist in the child. The body does not have a one-way door between a parent and
a child, which is why this route is the cheap one.)*

##### The population dynamics close the loop

Two populations, two sets of books:

| | source | sink |
|---|---|---|
| **shells in human hands** | limbs that did not transcend, **only at eclipses.** Rare | failed relocation: no carrier relative, a lineage that ends, a death too sudden for the driver to be ready |
| **limbs** | seeded by a shell under a parasite at an eclipse, then **seeded on by limbs** into kin and long partners | a carrier who seeds no one — and every carrier dies with theirs |

The limb population is the larger, slower one and it can recover; the shell
population only ever goes down between eclipses. Which means a lineage can keep
carrying limbs for centuries after the shell has gone, **with nobody in it
noticing anything is missing** — there was never anything to notice. A limb
without a shell has no faculty to lose.

So symbiote density is **a decayed record of past eclipse activity** — which is
exactly the [graveyard attractor](#what-draws-a-clutch-here). The island has
them because it has had eclipses *and* because its kinship structures are tight
enough for transmission to succeed.

##### The regulator is social, not geographic

**[proposal]** Nothing about the rock. Transmission needs **years of dense
physical contact within kin groups**, which is a property of societies:

> Symbiotes persist where human community is physically close and
> generationally continuous. They fail where it is atomised, mobile and
> mediated.

Which unifies with *why now — almost too late*: **modernity is hostile to the
parasite because it is hostile to symbiotes first.** The bridge has been quietly
dying back for a century. The island is one of the last reservoirs — not for
anything about the place, but because of how people there still live.

#### Minimal interference

**A symbiote interferes with its host as little as it can**, on an argument from
**energy conservation** — and consistently with [what it is scoring
for](MECHANICS.md#same-technique-different-goal), since perturbation is risk and
risk compounds over an open-ended horizon. It *nudges* toward outcomes it judges favourable. It
does not control minds. The human remains the author of their own choices
throughout.

Three things follow, and they are all load-bearing:

- **Humans keep their agency.** Neither species owns them. For a design that
  depends on the player coming to care about a small cast across many replays,
  this is not a detail — puppets are not sympathetic.
- **The town is just a town.** Nothing in the community reads as managed,
  because it is not being managed. It is being tended, lightly, by something
  spending as little as possible.
- **Losing a symbiote is survivable and mostly unremarkable.** It *feels*
  different — a guiding presence gone, a sense one cannot name — but for most
  humans it is neither obvious nor debilitating. **[open]** who the exceptions
  are: perhaps those a powerful old symbiote had been guiding heavily.

This restraint is also the yardstick the whole deviation system is measured
against — see [What counts as bad deviation](MECHANICS.md#what-counts-as-bad-deviation).

#### They recognise each other, and they form a network

**[proposal, strong]** A symbiote detects the presence and identity of another —
and the mechanism must stay **biochemical, never cognitive.** The precedents are
good: immune self/non-self recognition distinguishes foreign tissue with no mind
at all, and quorum sensing lets bacteria coordinate collective behaviour by
reading each other's signal density.

> **Language discipline:** signatures, calibration, thresholds, propagation.
> Never *decide*, *warn*, *agree* or *collaborate* in the sense of intent. This
> is coordination, not cooperation (see [They are not
> agents](#they-have-interests-but-they-are-not-agents)).

**The channel already exists.** When hosts touch, their symbiotes exchange
signatures over the same body-channel link everything else in this world runs
on. **Familiarity is a calibrated response to a known signature**; a newcomer is
an uncalibrated one. Calibration takes **centuries of contact**, and newcomers
are extremely rare.

##### Community scope is a network, not a faculty

This replaces a piece of hand-waving. "An old symbiote has community-wide scope"
asked for one organelle perceiving a whole village. It does not:

> **Deviation signal propagates symbiote-to-symbiote along the contact graph.**
> Old symbiotes in central, much-touched hosts are simply **better-connected
> nodes** — they integrate more because more reaches them.

Better biology, and it costs nothing: the contact graph is already in the model.

##### The parasite and the alarm share one wire

The parasite spreads by touch. The alarm propagates by touch. **They run on the
same network.**

> **Your transmission is their detection.** Every jump is also a signal moving
> outward, and the more aggressively you spread, the faster the island's
> regulatory system converges on the fact that something is happening.

The two things you most need to do are the two things that give you away, on the
same wire.

##### An old symbiote steers toward home

A symbiote's drivers are calibrated to a community — these people, this place.
Outside that network it resolves no signal and its models fit nothing, so it
keeps nudging toward conditions matching what it is tuned for.

> **A control system with a set point it cannot reach.** Mechanically: every
> branch it [scores](MECHANICS.md#the-objective-is-biomarkers-integrated-over-a-horizon)
> is scored with models calibrated elsewhere, so the ones trending homeward are
> the only ones it can evaluate at all. Not homesickness, and
> no agency required. It pushes toward the only configuration its models
> describe.

So **displaced old symbiotes self-correct**: either they return or their host
does. One is therefore never found far from where it was calibrated.

**A newly abandoned limb has no set point at all.** Holding a shell it has only
just inherited, with no community calibration, it has nothing to steer toward. *Adrift* is the
mechanically correct word.

> **Liability.** This must **not** become an explanation for indigenous
> attachment to land — a people's connection to their territory as partly
> parasitic would be grotesque, and the design is one careless sentence from it.
>
> Old symbiotes sit with **elders of every faction** (the cult spans all of
> them), so it is an elder phenomenon, not an indigenous one. Better still, make
> [the hotel owner](story/developers.md#the-hotel-owner) the illustration: she feels an
> inexplicable, disproportionate pull to the island and carries a symbiote taken
> from the matriarch's household. **The colonizer's daughter is held here by
> something taken from the colonized.**

##### A foreign signature does not resolve

An unrecognised symbiote is not integrated into the network — not for lack of
information, but because the signature does not resolve. Consequences:

- its deviation signal does not propagate; the collective does not read it
- it receives no collective signal either, so its scope collapses to its own host
- **calibration takes centuries, so a newcomer is foreign for life**

See [the young priest](story/church.md#the-young-priest), who is the island's only
instance.

##### The network is also a store

The contact graph does not only carry signal. **It holds state** — and that
state is readable, under rare conditions, by a human.

This must not quietly reintroduce agency. Symbiotes do not remember, record or
testify. What accumulates is **calibration**:

| a symbiote carries | which is |
|---|---|
| driver models for its host and its host's frequent contacts | how it nudges at all |
| registered signatures of every symbiote within contact range | how familiarity works |
| an integrated deviation baseline | what it regulates against |

Because signal propagates node to node, these states are **correlated across
the whole island.** The "distributed store" is nothing more than the network's
current configuration — the way tree rings are a record without anything having
written them down.

> **What it can answer:** who is connected to whom, how closely, which
> signatures are present, which are absent, which are foreign, and how long each
> has been calibrated — which gives rough age. Where deviation is coming from,
> right now.
>
> **What it cannot answer:** anything that required an event to be
> *represented.* No stories, no names, no deeds, no intentions. **There is no
> time index** — no *when*, only *how long calibrated.* Nothing in it is about
> anything. It is a configuration, not an account.

That constraint is the whole discipline. Every time a human reads this and comes
away with a narrative, **the narrative is the human's.**

##### Reading it requires a human in an altered state

The store is structurally closed to you. A parasite's signature does not resolve
(below), so you are outside the network and can read nothing from it directly —
not as a limitation of power but as the same fact that makes you invisible to it.

**The only known interface is a human practice**: sustained meditation plus
psychoactive plants, performed by someone whose own symbiote is a fully
calibrated node. It alters the host's processing until she can perceive what her
symbiote is configured as. See [the matriarch](story/islanders.md#the-practice-is-technique-not-spirituality).

> **The island's accumulated knowledge about your species is available to you
> only through a human who volunteers to go and get it.** You cannot take it.
> You can only arrange to be present when someone else does.

- **[open]** Whether this practice is rare because it is hard, because it is
  guarded, or because almost nobody has a symbiote old enough to be worth
  reading. The third is the cheapest and the most consistent.

#### Their restraint is costly to them

**[proposal]** If interference is minimised to conserve energy, then symbiotes
run a budget — and **resistance is expensive to them.** An old symbiote spending
itself to defend one host has less left over for the rest of its scope.

Which makes **forcing a symbiote to act an attack in its own right.** Provoke a
defence in one place to open a gap in another.

### They are not what you are

**A symbiote is a limb holding an abandoned shell.** Not a stage of your life
cycle, not a sibling, and not a failed version of anything —
**[a different organism entirely](#three-parties-and-one-of-them-inherits-the-equipment)**,
using equipment your lineage left behind.

Every symbiote trait follows from that:

| symbiote trait | because |
|---|---|
| samples timelines but does not author them | **the capability was always the shell's.** Authoring costs compute, and nothing in a limb's own programme ever justifies it |
| **local**, bound to one place | the shell is where it is, and the limb is tethered to it |
| spends as little as it can | perturbation is risk, and risk compounds over an indefinite horizon |
| protects its host community | **that is its habitat and its dispersal route.** Not regulation — self-interest |
| depends on humans | it lives in them |

> **Which preserves *same technique, different goal* in a better form.** It is
> not two species at two life stages. **It is one machine, driven by two
> different things, for two incompatible purposes** — and the deadline is still
> what makes one of them reckless.

#### They have interests, but they are not agents

**This needs more care than it used to.** A limb is an organism with a
reproductive future, so it has *interests* in a way a decommissioned organelle
does not. What it does not have is deliberation.

> **A gut bacterium has interests. It does not have opinions.**

It does not scheme, negotiate, hold grudges or want revenge. It is goal-directed
the way every living thing is goal-directed, and nothing above that.

Read every "wants", "protects" and "responds" in this document that way:

| reads as | actually is |
|---|---|
| the symbiote defends its host | an organism defending its habitat |
| the symbiote alerts humans | a symptom — the human feels wrong, like a fever |
| the symbiote escalates | a cheap defence failing, and a costlier one starting |
| the symbiote knows what you are | nothing knows anything |
| the symbiote chooses a branch | a scoring function returns an argmax |
| the shell decides who gets a symbiote | relocation lands where the score is highest |

> **Language discipline.** *Samples, scores, selects, commits, horizon, argmax.*
> Never *chooses*, *plans*, *anticipates* or *decides* — the machinery is
> [predictive, not
> deliberative](MECHANICS.md#same-technique-different-goal), and a model of the
> next few weeks is not a mind.

> **And the sentence that is now wrong:** *they are you, later, with nobody
> home.* They never were you. **They are what your lineage leaves behind in the
> creatures it uses** — still running, still inhabited, and now holding the
> instrument.

**This sorts out who the antagonist is.** Symbiotes are *physics* — the terrain,
an immune system, a cost function. **The only other agents in this world are
humans.** The late-game pressure has always been humans noticing that something
is riding them; the symbiote layer is what makes that pressure mount, not a
party to it.

It also settles a question asked earlier — does your species have a relationship
to theirs? Yes, but not a *social* one — **not kinship at all.** They are a
different lineage, living on what yours discards.

#### Two ways to end up a symbiote

- **The reserve runs out.** Forced, default, and what happened to every guardian
  on this island.
- **You stop before it does.** **[proposal]** Declining deliberately should
  differ from expiring: you pick your host, and — since the reserve is not
  exhausted — you are [a symbiote that kept a working
  body](#proposal-the-shell-retaining-symbiote), which has never happened.

##### It has happened here before

Totality recurs at a given location on the order of centuries: rare enough to be
legendary, frequent enough to sustain a tradition. Parasites have come here at
previous eclipses, and **every symbiote in this town is one of them, failed.**

So the folklore, the cults, the superstitions and the scientific curiosity are
all sediment from earlier attempts. The community has been a nursery for a very
long time and remembers it badly. The mystic's material is real; it is just old.

- **[proposal]** Total eclipses exist because the moon's apparent diameter
  almost exactly matches the sun's — a coincidence, and a receding one. If the
  lifecycle is timed to totality, Earth is a viable nursery only during a
  transient astronomical accident.
##### What draws a clutch here

**Symbiote density is the attractor, and the species generates it itself.**

Symbiotes are the bridge: no symbiote, no contact, so a seed landing where none
exist is inert on arrival. That makes their presence a **hard gate**, not a
preference. And density is highest wherever the species has failed most — which
is wherever conditions were historically best.

> **The species navigates by its own graveyard.** Not homing; gradient-following
> on a signal its own dead produce.

It drifts correctly, too. Symbiotes relocate when hosts die and populations
migrate, so viable sites move with human history. Nothing is place-bound. The
adult is simply optimising a conjunction: **totality × cognitive density ×
symbiote presence × a culture that still gathers.**

###### Which closes a loop

If symbiotes are expired parasites, the *first* arrivals had no bridge and
should have been inert on every world, forever.

**[proposal]** Unless **the direct link is the ancestral faculty** — how the
species operated before symbiotes existed, by bonding to unclaimed humans
directly.

That makes the late-game power the **oldest** one. The species became dependent
on infrastructure its own failures built, and the direct link is atavistic: hard
to reach now not because it is advanced but because it has been disused for a
hundred million years.

##### Why here, and why now

**The seed was placed.** An adult has a vast view into spacetime; aiming at a
particular eclipse is trivial for it. So the question is not whether it was
deliberate but what the criteria were.

**A puzzle the rules create:** adults scatter seeds *far apart*, because merging
works best when zones of influence do not overlap — yet this town has been
seeded repeatedly, which is where its symbiotes came from.

> **Resolution: it is not the same parent.** An adult spreads its own offspring
> widely and has no reason to coordinate with rivals. Viable nurseries are rare,
> so unrelated adults converge on the same few sites across centuries. The town
> is not one lineage's garden — it is contested ground.

- **[open]** Which leaves the option that **another seed is here now.** Noted,
  not committed.

**Why here** — three criteria, in order of force:

1. **Symbiotes are the infrastructure.** No symbiote means no bridge, so a seed
   landing where none exist wakes unable to touch anything and dies inert. A
   parasite can only operate where its predecessors already failed.

   > **The nursery is made of the dead.** Every previous failure left behind
   > exactly the organ the next arrival needs. This place is viable *because* so
   > many have died here. The symbiotes are not only the opposition — they are
   > the soil.

2. **Concentration beats population.** The leash is small. A remote town that
   gathers on one hillside is worth more than a city where a million people
   watch from a million windows. Headcount outside the radius is worth nothing.
3. **Totality must pass here**, which is rare, and rarer still in combination
   with the first two.

**Why now** — and the answer is *almost too late*:

- **Human cognitive density is at a historical peak.** More educated, better
  connected minds than at any previous eclipse.
- **But physical gathering and touch are in decline.** The parasite needs skin
  contact and crowds; late modernity is mediated, distanced, indoors, apart. A
  community that still holds festivals, still goes to church, still touches is
  among the **last** viable nurseries rather than a backwater.
- **[proposal]** In deep time the moon recedes and totality ends altogether. The
  species is running out of Earth from both ends at once.


#### The shells are in the fossil record

**They are not remains.** A shell does not die and does not degrade; it goes
quiet. Shells have been accumulating on Earth for a very long time, and **an
unknown fraction of them still anchor living limbs** walking around inside
people.

> **The fossil record of this taxon is a population, not a graveyard**, and
> nothing in the rock tells the two apart.

**Paleontology already has a category for it: *problematica*** — fossils that
resist classification. The model is *Tullimonstrum gregarium*, the Tully
Monster: Carboniferous, essentially one Illinois locality, still contested as to
whether it is even a vertebrate, and the state fossil regardless. A taxon can be
well-published and wholly unexplained for a century.

The tells a specialist would find quietly wrong:

- **no lineage** — it appears fully formed, with no ancestors and no descendants
- **no juveniles** — no developmental series, ever
- **irregular temporal distribution** — clusters, not continuity
- **biogeography matching nothing**

> **The correlation nobody sane would look for:** fossil localities against
> historical eclipse paths. A career-ending idea, requiring exactly the
> interdisciplinary accident a small mixed-discipline station produces. A far better route into the truth than deducing the parasite
> directly.

##### Deep time, and what it implies

**Humans are not uniquely suited — only currently best.** Compute is **group
size × per-individual cognition**, and the parasite can work either end of that
product. A troop of primates and a vast herd of something far stupider are both
solutions, in different shapes. Humans are one extreme: very few individuals,
enormous capability each.

So Earth has been seeded for hundreds of millions of years, and the record is
not uniform failure — it is the species working **progressively better
substrates** as social animals became more social. The earliest arrivals woke to
nothing and went inert. Later ones found something.

> Not a species discovering Earth, but a long, patient, mostly unsuccessful
> campaign that has slowly become more possible. The deep-time version of [Why
> here, and why now](#why-here-and-why-now).

##### We are what the failures left behind

**[proposal, strong]** The parasite may be one of the drivers of the otherwise
unexplained human cognitive explosion — **but the mechanism is the symbiotes,
not the parasites.**

A parasite arrives, fails, and leaves a symbiote. Symbiotes nudge their hosts
toward viability: mildly beneficial, no intent, pure homeostasis. And they
**transmit only through years of dense kin contact, and persist only in socially
continuous groups** (see [How symbiotes persist between
eclipses](#how-symbiotes-persist-between-eclipses)).

That is a selection pressure with a direction. Lineages carrying symbiotes do
marginally better, and the traits that permit transmission are **tight social
bonds, high physical contact, long childhoods**, and the cognition to support
them.

> **The symbiotes bred us for sociality.** Not deliberately — they are
> organelles. Differential survival, over millions of years. And sociality
> dragged cognition up behind it.

Which closes into a loop:

> limbs fail to transcend → symbiotes accumulate → symbiotes select for
> sociality and long childhoods → cognition rises → better substrate → more
> seeds arrive → more stay → more symbiotes

**Humanity's cognitive explosion is the byproduct of a very long accumulation of
limbs that never left.**

#### Custodianship: what the organism actually needs from us

**This is the reason for everything else.**

A shell with tethered limbs is an **occupied body that cannot move, cannot
defend itself and cannot feed itself.** It needs:

| | |
|---|---|
| **durable physical protection** | across geological time, against weather, predation, burial and breakage |
| **periodic contact** | it stores state, not energy, and state kept in spin degrees of freedom has to be refreshed by a limb that comes back |
| **continuity** | a group that is still there in a thousand years |

> So the organism's interest is in **long-term viable groups of individuals who
> will look after the shell** — and it has used whatever was available, in
> whatever place and era, for hundreds of millions of years. Most of it was not
> very good at the job.

##### Humans are the best custodians that have ever existed

Not because of cognition, or not only. Because of what cognition built:

- **Permanent dwellings** and the habit of keeping things in them
- **Heirlooms** — objects deliberately passed down a family for generations
- **Collections**, catalogues, climate control, insurance
- **Institutions** that outlive every individual in them
- **Law** that protects objects designated as heritage

**No previous substrate could do any of that.** A herd cannot archive. Humans
keep strange stones on shelves for four hundred years and then donate them to a
museum.

##### Which is why this island

The [existing criteria](#why-here-and-why-now) — symbiotes already present,
totality passing, a leash that rewards concentration — are the ordinary ones.
**This site has something rarer.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **[the developer's fossil collection](story/developers.md#he-collected-fossils-hunted-and-kept-an-amateur-naturalists-eye)** | a private cabinet in a climate-controlled villa. Shells gathered from across the island into one protected room |
| **[the research station](story/station.md#the-stations-fracture)** | an institution with an archive, a remit covering local scientific peculiarities, and a mandate to continue |

> **Humans on this island have independently begun doing exactly what the
> organism needs**, for reasons entirely their own — a rich man's hobby and a
> small institution's collections policy. **It is the best protection a shell has
> ever been offered**, and an adult aiming a seed can see it.

##### What collecting shells actually does

**[proposal, strong]** A shell is the anchor of every limb still tethered to it.
So moving one moves a symbiote's leash.

- The developer, assembling his collection, was **gathering the bodies of the
  island's guardian spirits into his house** — without knowing, and without
  anyone being able to tell him.
- **The relics in households** are not souvenirs. A family keeps an odd stone on
  a shelf, and the thing that looks after them is tethered to it.
- **[The cabinet is now in the hotel owner's
  house](story/developers.md#she-is-the-hub-of-the-endgame)**, which means the anchors are
  too.

- **[open]** What displacement does to a tethered limb — drag, stress, loss of
  homing, or nothing at all. Something should be observable, because [the
  matriarch can read the network](story/islanders.md#the-practice-is-technique-not-spirituality)
  and the collection was assembled in living memory.

It makes *the nursery is made of the dead* literal at species scale: the dead
did not only leave the bridge behind, they **raised the crop.** The parasite
arriving now is harvesting something its own ancestors' failures cultivated
without ever meaning to. And humans stay unspecial — not chosen, just the
current best substrate, and that only because of accumulated debris.

###### The liability

This sits one inch from **"ancient aliens uplifted humanity"** — a tired trope
with unpleasant baggage.

What makes it categorically different is that **there is no intent, no gift and
no teacher.** Nobody taught us anything; nobody chose us. A selection pressure
exerted by discarded infrastructure is not uplift — it is closer to how grazing
animals shape grassland.

**The moment anything implies purpose or benevolence it collapses into the
trope.** So: never confirm it, and never let it be *given*. The station's
anthropologist can hold a theory about the cognitive explosion that happens to
be correct, and should be treated by colleagues the way such theories actually
are.

##### Humans are CPUs, not batteries

A useful correction to the obvious reading, and the design has since gone
further: **there is no fuel term at all.** The shell supplies power; humans
supply processing; nothing is burned.

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **the shell** | power and storage | **portable, finite, yours** |
| **human minds** | processing | **not portable, not stored, not yours** — available only while a mind is in a state that releases it |

That is the precise reason the transcendence event requires physical proximity:
**collective effervescence is the bus.** A coherent computation cannot run on
minds that are not coupled — and emotion is what makes a mind coherent enough to
couple at all.

And a borrowed processor does not notice. Nobody is drained. They are briefly,
unknowingly, running something else's process on their spare cycles.

##### Two classes of remains

| | age | who holds it |
|---|---|---|
| **fossil husks** | deep time; failures with nothing to inhabit | the scientific record — published, legitimate, unexplained |
| **recent husks** | the last few thousand years, at eclipse sites | folklore. A relic in a church, "the stone that fell" |

> **The scientist's problematic fossil and the mystic's relic are the same
> object, and neither knows.** The two exposition channels are looking at one
> thing from opposite ends, unable to see each other — [two wrong
> models](story/learning.md#two-wrong-models-no-adjudication) made physical.


## The mechanism

> **Strictly authorial.** The entity has no concepts and the game states none of
> this. It is written down so that the rules elsewhere in these documents are
> consequences of one picture rather than a list of stipulations, and so that
> [the grounding stays
> superficial](MECHANICS.md#the-superficial-grounding-which-is-deliberately-not-load-bearing)
> on purpose rather than by accident.

### Three parties, and only one of them is the entity's own matter

| | what it is | what it supplies |
|---|---|---|
| **the shell** | a mineral object, phosphatic — calcium phosphate | **memory.** The master copy of every driver |
| **the limb** | molecular apparatus built out of host proteins, distributed through the host's tissue | **the operation.** It is the actuator |
| **the host** | a person | **everything else** — the computation, the energy, the substrate, the body |

> **The entity contributes a recipe and an aim. The host pays for all of it.**

The shell is not a battery and never was. It stores no energy and transmits
none. It holds state.

### The transaction

The underlying protocol is **quantum energy teleportation** (Hotta, 2008):
given two neighbouring regions of a field whose ground state is entangled, a
measurement in the first plus a *classical message* lets a conditional operation
in the second extract energy from the second region's own local vacuum.

Mapped onto a host:

| QET | here |
|---|---|
| **Alice** — measures locally, injects energy | one site of the limb's apparatus |
| **the classical message** | ordinary electrochemistry, microns away |
| **Bob** — applies the conditional operation, extracts | another site of the limb's apparatus, on the target membrane |
| **the entangled ground state** | the vacuum's own, between two adjacent regions of the same tissue |

> **Both ends are inside the host, microns apart.** The shell is not a party to
> the transaction.

#### Why both ends have to be in the host

Vacuum entanglement between two regions collapses with separation — microns,
with any realistic biological coupling. Not metres, and certainly not
kilometres. A shell in a cabinet cannot be Alice for a brain across the island,
and no amount of apparatus fixes that.

Which settles what the entity is actually selling:

> **It does not supply energy. It supplies aim.** The host's brain has ample
> energy and no way to place a deposit at one specific cell in one specific
> millisecond. That placement is the entire product.

#### The ledger, which runs through the host

QET is **net-lossy by construction** — the injection at Alice always exceeds the
extraction at Bob, or it would be a perpetual motion machine. Both ends are in
the host, so **the loss is paid out of the host's metabolism.**

This makes an accounting identity out of something stated elsewhere as a design
principle: the energy spent is the host's, and spending it is exactly what moves
the biomarkers [the symbiotes
score](MECHANICS.md#the-objective-is-biomarkers-integrated-over-a-horizon). [Cost
and exposure are the same
measurement](MECHANICS.md#cost-and-exposure-are-the-same-measurement) is not an
analogy.

### What the operation is, concretely

**Conformational change in molecular structure** — the same thing every protein
in the body already does. The limb is molecular apparatus sitting on membranes,
and its operations are shape changes in those molecules, gated by barriers of a
few *kT* and driven by the thermal bath that is already present.

**No photons.** Emission would be a transmission, and transmissions can be found.

#### It is a gate, not a push

This resolves the circularity the architecture otherwise has — the limb appearing
to need the energy it is trying to obtain in order to obtain it.

> A valve does not supply the water pressure. It gets turned, and turning it
> costs arbitrarily less than the flow it releases.

Bob's operation is a **control** operation. Nothing in the physics requires a
control to cost what it gates. Laboratory apparatus does, because laboratory
apparatus is clumsy; nothing says it must.

The irreducible floor is **Landauer's principle**: erasing one bit costs at least
*kT* ln2, dissipated as heat. Computing is free in principle; *forgetting* is
not.

```
Landauer floor, per bit        3 × 10⁻²¹ J   (body temperature)
delivered, per neuron          1 × 10⁻¹⁶ J
```

The limb runs in the black as long as deciding to flip one neuron costs fewer
than ~33,000 bits of erasure. Real biological molecular machines run 20–25 *kT*
per step, some thirty times above the Landauer limit — which the five orders of
magnitude of headroom absorbs without complaint.

### Why the apparatus has to be coherent, which is not about entanglement

The point most easily got wrong, and the one everything else rests on.

> **No part of the energy transaction is entangled with the shell.**

That is a statement about *this* protocol, not about the organism — a symbiote's
limb [is entangled with its shell](#the-eclipse-is-what-creates-the-tether), and
that is where its branch-sampling comes from. It simply plays no part in
delivering energy.

The entanglement the protocol consumes is **in the field, between two
neighbouring regions of space.** It is a free, universal property of the vacuum.
Nobody establishes it, nobody ships it, and it cannot be exhausted. Alice's
instrument and Bob's instrument are instruments sitting in those regions, not
partners in a correlation.

Coherence is required for an entirely different reason: **control fidelity.**

> **A random operation adds energy. Only the specified one extracts it.**

Bob must apply a particular unitary, phase included. A decohered actuator applies
a randomised phase and on average pumps energy *into* the local field rather than
drawing it out. The coherence is not a channel to anywhere — it is the difference
between an instrument and a kick.

The same reason a laser must be coherent to manipulate a quantum state: not
because it is entangled with its target, but because it has a defined phase.

#### Where the coherence comes from, which is nowhere far

Nothing is transported. The limb manufactures what it needs **on site, out of
the host's own metabolites.**

The mechanism is borrowed intact from Matthew Fisher's *Quantum Cognition*
(2015): pyrophosphatase hydrolyses pyrophosphate into two phosphate ions, and
the symmetry of that reaction leaves the two phosphorus-31 nuclear spins in an
entangled singlet state. Posner molecules — Ca₉(PO₄)₆ — then shield the result.
An ordinary enzyme, an ordinary metabolite, continuous production as a byproduct
of normal chemistry.

So the limb needs **a recipe, not a delivery.** Which is also why the shell can
be a mineral: phosphatic shells are a real fossil category, and the organism's
body and its hosts' spin carriers are then the same chemistry.

Fisher's chain also supplies the step from quantum state to firing: the Posner
molecules enter the presynaptic neuron, break up, release calcium, and change
neurotransmitter release — which changes the postsynaptic cell's probability of
firing.

> **[open]** Fisher's hypothesis is unconfirmed and disputed. In-vitro chemistry
> supports parts of it; nobody has observed it in a living brain. For these
> purposes that status is ideal and no stronger claim is needed.

### What the energy is for, with numbers

Not powering neurons — tipping ones already near threshold. The host's own
metabolism pays for every spike.

```
C ≈ 200 pF           whole-cell capacitance, cortical pyramidal neuron
ΔV ≈ 1–5 mV          distance to threshold for a cell sitting elevated

E = ½C(ΔV)²  =  10⁻¹⁶ J per neuron at 1 mV
```

An action potential costs ~10⁻¹¹ J, so **the neuron amplifies the trigger by a
factor of 100,000.**

For an emotionally loaded decision, take the engaged circuit — amygdala, vmPFC,
anterior cingulate, insula, ventral striatum — at ~10⁸ neurons:

| what | cost |
|---|---|
| tip one marginal neuron | 10⁻¹⁶ J |
| a flicker of attention | ~10⁻¹³ J |
| a noticeable mood shift | ~10⁻¹¹ J |
| **a drastic decision** (25% of the circuit) | **~10⁻⁸ J** |

Ten nanojoules is **half a nanosecond of the brain's own 20 W budget**, or about
what it takes to drop a grain of sand one millimetre. A single AA battery holds
enough for a trillion of them.

Two things follow that the design already wanted:

- **Eight orders of magnitude of dynamic range**, with the largest push costing
  ten million times the smallest. The arithmetic favours many small sustained
  pushes, for arithmetic reasons.
- **Arousal is leverage, not just bandwidth.** In a recurrent network near
  criticality, flipping a fraction of a percent cascades to the rest. A calm
  brain makes you buy all 25%; a near-critical one makes you buy 0.1% and lets
  the avalanche do the remainder — the same outcome, a few hundred times
  cheaper. This is [emotional state as a double
  multiplier](MECHANICS.md#emotional-state-is-a-double-multiplier), derived.

#### The ladder

```
a bit of information          ~10⁻²¹ J   paid by the thermal bath
        ↓  ×10⁵
energy at the membrane        ~10⁻¹⁶ J   paid by the local vacuum
        ↓  ×10⁵
the neuron's own spike        ~10⁻¹¹ J   paid by the host's metabolism
        ↓  ×10³–10⁵
the decision                  ~10⁻⁸ J    paid by the host's own network
```

Four stages, each paid for by something other than the entity.

### The channels

Three of them, and conflating the first two is the standing error.

| | what moves | medium | timescale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. establishment** | **a seed population of units**, carrying the codebook and the model with them | physical transfer by sustained contact | years |
| **2. operational** | Alice's measurement outcome | ordinary electrochemistry inside one skull | microseconds, microns |
| **3. back to the shell** | **for a parasite, nothing** — it makes no decision that would require knowing. **For a symbiote, the units themselves**, carrying their half of the correlation | contact-limited. Somebody has to handle the shell |

#### A driver is a codebook

QET needs exactly one host-specific object: a lookup from **Alice-side
measurement outcome → which conditional operation Bob applies.** That is what a
driver is — **held materially**, in the unit population and archived in the
shell, never stored as a file anywhere.

Which puts [the shell's two kinds of
spending](MECHANICS.md#the-shell-is-the-budget-and-it-has-two-kinds-of-spending)
in the right units. A codebook has a size; it occupies capacity permanently,
which is why `shellCost` is never refunded — and **a relative's codebook is an
edit of one already held**, not a new one, which is the whole of the vertical
transmission argument.

The establishment channel also carries the *model* of the host — the `horizon`
parameter in [push
admissibility](MECHANICS.md#feasibility-is-three-conditions-not-one). All of it
travels the way [a microbiome
travels](#why-family-and-the-mechanism-is-the-same-one): by contact, slowly, and
only into a body that has been read first.

#### The eclipse is what creates the tether

A parasite is not tethered. A symbiote is. **The eclipse is the difference**, and
it is the only moment at which it could be.

Establishing fresh entanglement between a shell and a host needs mass proximity,
synchrony and coherence all at once — which happens exactly once, at [the
event](MECHANICS.md#the-one-place-bandwidth-matters), and nowhere else in the
organism's life.

> **The event that was supposed to free it is what binds it.** The tether is a
> product of the failure.

##### Which is what "tethered" means, with nothing flowing

A reasonable objection: what is a tether, if nothing travels along it? The answer
is that nothing does.

> **A tethered limb is not connected to the shell. It is part of it.**

One quantum system, not two in communication. Operations on the limb act on the
joint state, which is not signalling — it is being a single object. Severing is
not cutting a wire; it is amputation.

##### And it is where the symbiote's whole faculty comes from

Sampling branches and selecting among them requires coherence spanning branches,
and [only the shell spans branches](#across-timelines). A limb in warm tissue
cannot.

> **Cut from its shell, a symbiote is not a symbiote. It is a thing in a person,
> running forward in time like everything else.**

No sampling, no selection, no horizon, no regulation. The one faculty that
separates it from ordinary biochemistry is borrowed from the body it is attached
to.

**This also settles the leash.** Being one system imposes no range limit —
entanglement does not decay with distance, it decays with environmental
interaction. So separation costs a *rate*, not a cliff:

> **A symbiote far from its shell does not stop working. It loses coherence
> faster, and with it the faculty, slowly.**

Which is better than the hard limit it replaces: it explains why displaced old
symbiotes self-correct, why one is never found far from where it was made, and
why nothing dramatic happens when a relic is carried to the next village.

#### The parasite has no back channel

**Entanglement carries no information, in either direction** — the
no-communication theorem is a theorem, not an engineering limit. But the
stronger result is that nothing needs to come back at all.

**The shell never learns anything from a host, because it never makes a decision
that would require knowing.** It plants an initial specification and the batches
in which that specification produced the wanted outcome are the ones kept. There
is no mid-course correction to compute, so there is no reading to do.

> **It is not an engineer. It is a breeder.** It does not design a chain that
> works — it plants many and keeps the ones that ran. The failures were simply
> not kept.

Which is the deep form of a rule already stated: [it does not wait, does not plan
ahead, does not find out](MECHANICS.md#the-parasite-experiences-the-whole-timeline-at-once).

##### The bandwidth problem dissolves

Continuous decision-making would need a live, high-rate link from every host.
Planting a specification needs a one-time transfer. **A letter, not a
telephone** — and the letter is the establishment channel that already exists.

##### Drivers are selected, not learned

> **Nothing acquires better drivers. The batches in which better drivers happened
> to have been produced are the ones kept.**

This stays compatible with *driver quality is bought with compute*, because
compute buys **selective pressure** rather than learning: more of it means more
can be conditioned on, so more of what survives is what was wanted. Progression
by selection, which is the right verb for this organism.

**The fold is the write.** The shell's state after a fold reflects which batch
survived, because the shell is what spans the branches being measured. No
channel — the measurement is the update.

##### The symbiote is the exception, and the reason is principled

A symbiote **does** have a back channel. It is physical, high-latency, and made
of the units themselves.

> **Selection does not require storage. Persistence does.**

The parasite has a deadline and a transcendence to reach, so planting and
selecting is all it needs; reading anything back would buy it nothing. A symbiote
has no deadline and its entire existence *is* persistence across centuries — so
it needs an archive, and an archive needs writing to.

**The units carry it.** Each one holds phosphorus nuclear spins paired with the
shell, so moving a unit physically moves its half of the correlation. That is the
only kind of channel that could exist, since nothing can be *sent*.

> **It requires physically handling the shell.** The sync operation is somebody
> picking the relic up.

Which gives the ceremonial handling of relics a mechanical function that nobody
performing it knows about. **The matriarch's custodial role is custodial in a
literal sense**, and the rites that call for a stone to be taken out and held are
maintenance.

> **What the cabinet actually did.** [Collecting the
> shells](#what-collecting-shells-actually-does) did something worse than
> relocate them. **It ended the handling.** Those relics sat in households being
> picked up — at rites, by children — for centuries, and now they are behind
> glass in a stranger's house and nobody touches them. A slow severance of
> several guardians from their archives: invisible, uninstrumentable, and
> entirely the result of a rich man's hobby being tidier than a family's shelf.

##### Shells do not coordinate. They converge

Two shells sampling the same world and scoring it with the same objective select
the same branches, for the same reason two calculators agree. **Shared objective
plus shared evidence produces identical behaviour with nothing passing between
them.**

So the island's guardians act as one without any mechanism for acting as one —
instant rather than slow, undetectable because there is nothing to detect, and
non-agentic because nothing was ever told anything.

> **What differs between them is the horizon, and that is enough to produce
> everything that looks like politics.** An old symbiote prices a long span, so
> what scores best for its host is what keeps the group standing. A young one
> prices a short span, so what scores best is what benefits its host now. They
> diverge without disagreeing, because neither is doing anything but arithmetic.

#### What entanglement can do instead

It cannot send a message. It **can** let two parties produce the *same*
unpredictable sequence with no channel between them — neither chooses the value;
both get it. That is the basis of quantum key distribution.

> **[proposal] Two limbs holding shared entangled pairs act in lockstep with
> nothing passing between them.** Not signalling. Correlation, drawn from a stock
> laid down when they were made.

| property | consequence |
|---|---|
| finite, inherited, non-renewable | spent pairs do not come back |
| undetectable by construction | there is no channel to find |
| **cannot coordinate on anything chosen** | only on shared randomness. It buys synchrony, never command |

The last row is the one to protect: the island's guardians can act as one without
any of them deciding anything, which is the behaviour the setting wants and the
ontology [it has committed to](#they-have-interests-but-they-are-not-agents), at once.

It also hands the station the right wrong answer — correlation across the island
with no transmission vector and no contact path. Right that something is
coordinated, wrong that anything was sent.

### Across timelines

Branches in Everett do not communicate because **decoherence** suppresses
interference between them — suppressed, not forbidden. The off-diagonal terms go
to approximately zero, not to zero, and the branches remain in the universal
wavefunction. What blocks cross-branch anything is leakage to the environment.

Which means the exemption already granted covers this too, and no second one is
needed:

> **Only the shell spans branches.** Crystalline, isolated, nuclear-spin-based —
> the one part of the organism that could. A limb in a warm brain cannot, and
> neither can a host.

That is why the shell sits at the root of the timeline tree, why [folding is a
shell operation](MECHANICS.md#the-timeline-is-a-draft-until-you-fold-it), and why
a symbiote [samples timelines it cannot
author](MECHANICS.md#same-technique-different-goal) — its shell is still an
anchor and can no longer run the expensive operation.

#### Frameworks to lean on, none of them invented here

| | what it gives |
|---|---|
| **Deutsch 1985** | a protocol for an observer kept perfectly coherent to demonstrate another branch was real. Its sole requirement is isolation |
| **Deutsch 1991** (closed timelike curves) | the grandfather paradox resolved by a fixed-point density matrix, read Everettianly: **the curve connects branches, not one branch to itself.** Available as flavour; see the warning below before taking its computational claims |
| **Two-state vector formalism** (Aharonov, Bergmann & Lebowitz 1964) | a system described by a forward state *and* a backward state from post-selection. *The parasite experiences the whole timeline at once*, as a formalism. Weak measurement comes with it — information without collapse |
| **Post-selected CTCs** (Lloyd et al., 2011) | entanglement plus post-selection on a Bell measurement. Post-selection **is** folding |
| **Transactional interpretation** (Cramer 1986) | every event a handshake between a forward offer wave and a backward confirmation — good imagery for a two-boundary state. Single-world and collapse-based, so **use the picture, not the formalism** |
| **ER = EPR** (Maldacena & Susskind 2013) | entanglement **is** a wormhole — non-traversable, which is why it carries nothing. Gao, Jafferis & Wall 2017 make it traversable by coupling the sides, which is sending a classical signal. **A traversable wormhole is quantum teleportation** |

The last row is the one to note: the architecture above — a bridge of correlation
that is useless alone, made usable by a classical message — has a gravitational
dual. That was not arranged.

#### Interference is not communication

The one place this is easy to get wrong, because two true statements look like
they contradict each other.

| | forbids | permits |
|---|---|---|
| **no-signalling** | one party *choosing* what another receives | — |
| **quantum computation** | — | one coherent system processing a superposition and letting the amplitudes **interfere** |

A quantum computer does use the branches — that is Deutsch's own argument for
Everett. But no branch sends anything to another. **They recombine.** Amplitudes
from every branch arrive at a common output, wrong answers cancel, right ones
reinforce, and the pattern of cancellation is the computation.

> **The branches do not talk. They merge, and the merge is what computes.**

Which is the same shape as everything else here: correlation alone is always
inert, and what pays is what is done locally with it.

##### Which constrains what the entity can know

It cannot learn *"in that branch, she said yes."* Reading a specific alternative
is messaging. What it gets is what quantum algorithms actually give — **global
properties of the whole space**: whether a branch of some kind exists, how the
outcome space is structured, where amplitude concentrates.

> **It perceives structure, not episodes.** The shape of the possibility space,
> never the content of any particular alternative. A gradient, not a library.

##### Keep the exemption to one

Not all of the frameworks above cost the same, and they must not be treated as
interchangeable:

| assumption | buys | cost |
|---|---|---|
| **coherence maintained** | ordinary quantum computation (**BQP**) | **the one exemption, already granted** |
| Deutsch CTCs exist | PSPACE | actual closed timelike curves — a second miracle |
| P-CTCs exist | PP | post-selection as a physical primitive — also a second |

BQP is sufficient. Evaluating the structure of an enormous possibility space in a
way no classical process could is the entire premise, and it comes free with the
exemption already taken.

##### And there is no causality violation inside a branch

[Push admissibility](MECHANICS.md#feasibility-is-three-conditions-not-one) never
breaks causality: the computation happens **forward, in host brains, in their
linear time**, and the test only asks whether there were cycles in the window
before the moment. The player chooses non-linearly; the fiction runs forward.

For the shell's own position outside linear time, the formalism is the two-state
vector one — a state fixed by boundary conditions at **both** ends of the run,
awakening and the eclipse. Nothing travels backward; the description is two-sided.

> **Post-selection is not control.** Which branch survives is not chosen. Only
> the ones that do are got — and that is exactly what a fold does. It selects; it
> never authors backward.

##### Where the quantum part physically runs

If hosts were purely classical there would be no cross-branch computation
anywhere. Fisher's proposal already answers it: the processing is in neural
tissue, on Posner-protected ³¹P spins. **The limb organises the host's own
phosphorus into the register**, so the host supplies the classical thinking and
the quantum substrate both, while the shell supplies the coherence reference and
the memory.

**This is not a second currency.** Compute is compute; that some of it is
coherent explains why the entity can do what it does and is not a second meter.
[One resource and one rate](MECHANICS.md#the-economy-is-one-resource-and-one-rate)
stands.

#### What multiplicity buys

Everything many branches give over one is **many-to-one**, and none of it needs
the many to communicate. That is exactly why it is permitted.

| mechanism | what it gives |
|---|---|
| **amplitude amplification** (Grover; Brassard–Høyer–Mosca–Tapp) | O(1/√p) coherent rounds turn success probability *p* into near-certainty. It **reweights which branch is arrived in** — steering, not reading |
| **magic state distillation** (Bravyi & Kitaev 2005) | many noisy copies → one clean one. The unlock is a **kind**, not an amount |
| **the threshold theorem** | below a noise rate, redundancy buys unbounded coherent computation; above it, nothing helps at all |
| **measurement-based computation** (Raussendorf & Briegel) | the resource is prepared in advance; consuming it *is* computing, with classical feedforward only |

All four require the branches to be **still coherent**. Which locates the real
expense: what the shell spends itself on is not thinking, it is *not
decohering*. A branch that leaks is not abandoned so much as deleted from the
resource.

##### Distillation, which is what the fold is

Clifford operations alone are **classically simulable** (Gottesman–Knill) — a
machine restricted to them does nothing a classical process could not. Magic
states are what make it universal, and noisy ones can be purified:

1. **Entangle** the noisy copies with a Clifford circuit.
2. **Measure most of them.** Those measurements are the checks.
3. If the checks come back clean, **keep the one that was never measured.**
4. If they do not, **discard the entire batch.**

> **The copies are spent verifying each other, and the survivor is the one
> nobody looked at.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **15-to-1** (Reed–Muller code) | cubic suppression, *p* → ~35*p*³. Fixed point at *p* ≈ 0.17 |
| **5-to-1** (five-qubit code) | quadratic suppression. Fewer inputs, weaker purification, so more rounds |

Three properties, all load-bearing:

- **Many-to-one by construction.** One copy cannot be distilled, however good it
  is. One run is Clifford.
- **A hard fidelity floor.** Below it no quantity helps; above it the process
  converges. A phase transition, not a curve.
- **The magic is already in every copy.** Distillation creates nothing — it takes
  something too corrupted to use and makes it clean enough. **Every sibling had
  the thing; none of them could use it.**

#### The line that holds everywhere

Every framework listed respects **no-signalling.** None of them lets anything be
sent without a classical channel alongside it.

> **A being that exists across every timeline and still cannot tell anyone
> anything.**

Which is why the shells still talk through people, why coordination is still
contact-limited, and why the station cannot find a transmitter.

> **[proposal] Branch weight.** Born-rule amplitude is a measure over branches,
> so timelines are not equal — some carry more. A creature acting across them
> would care about weight rather than existence, and folding becomes a
> reweighting rather than a merge.

### The one exemption

Everything above runs on real physics with real numbers. One thing does not.

> **The organism maintains nuclear-spin coherence in warm tissue, and uses it.**

Not a large miracle. Nuclear spins genuinely do hold coherence for seconds in
warm liquid — it is why NMR works at room temperature without a cryostat, and
why Fisher's proposal is debated rather than dismissed. The exemption is not
*that coherence is possible* but *that something evolved to exploit it*.

Four hundred million years of being a shell is what that took, and it is the only
law the setting breaks.

## The moral structure

### [open] Should transcendence be reachable at low deviation?

The largest open question in the design. Two answers, and they are different
games.

| | **A — reachable benignly** | **B — transcendence requires harm** |
|---|---|---|
| the routes | [festival, beach and observance](MECHANICS.md#the-forms-it-can-take) all work; [integration beats suppression](MECHANICS.md#integration-is-the-other-way-to-remove-opposition-and-it-is-the-better-one) | only coercive configurations reach the threshold |
| the species | not strictly parasitic — it can coexist | genuinely parasitic; reproduction requires damage |
| the symbiote ending | a counterweight | a choice |
| the benign content | can win | cannot win |

#### A third position

Under either answer, **transcendence collapses every other timeline**. A benign
run harms nobody in the surviving line and ends every other version of every
person in it — so the goal can be interrogated without requiring harm along the
way.

And **some of what a clean run requires can only be acquired in a dirty one**
(see [the reverse competence gate](MECHANICS.md#and-the-gate-runs-the-other-way-too)):
you cannot amplify someone into a state you have never seen in them, and
extremes do not surface in ordinary weeks. [The fold launders
it](MECHANICS.md#the-fold-is-a-laundering-operation) — deviation does not
survive, drivers do.

#### A difficulty-curve variant, compatible with either

**Low-deviation transcendence may be out of reach on the early seeds** — not
morally gated, just not achievable without the drivers and reach that folding
accumulates. Early benign attempts fall short; late ones succeed.

### The symbiote ending

**Available from the first run.** Stay, bonded, local, small — give up the power
to rewrite in exchange for remaining.

**[proposal]** Make it capable of ending the **campaign**, not merely the run,
so that stopping is an authored ending rather than closing the game.

### What abandoning transcendence costs the species

Arithmetic, not sentiment:

- **Transcendence is the only route to adulthood**, and [reproduction is two
  adults merging timelines](#the-parasite-you). A larva that stops never
  reproduces; there is no other route.
- **Symbiosis is a demographic sink.** [The symbiote population is
  conserved](#how-symbiotes-persist-between-eclipses) — created only by failed
  parasites, lost to failed transitions, never growing. Every larva that stays
  is a line that ends.
- **The seed was placed deliberately**, by an adult that could see far enough
  into spacetime to aim. **[proposal]** Somewhere a parent's reach ends short — a
  timeline that never arrives at a merge, with nothing present to notice.

> **If every larva declined, the species ends.** The transcendence event is the
> only thing this kind of life does to continue.

**Keep the framing out of the entity.** The species has no morality, no loyalty
and no concepts; it cannot betray anything, because there is nothing it is
faithful to.

### The mutualism

**[proposal, strong]** Symbiosis is not only a failure state. What a guardian
does for a person:

| | |
|---|---|
| **protects them**, [regulating toward their good and their community's](MECHANICS.md#what-they-regulate-toward) | the whole of what it does |
| **interferes minimally**, by its own standard | the host keeps their agency entirely |
| **persists across generations**, moving into a relative | a household holds one for centuries |
| **connects them** to [a network and a store](#the-network-is-also-a-store) | which [one woman on this island can read](story/islanders.md#the-practice-is-technique-not-spirituality) |

A thousand years of stable mutual benefit, on an island where nobody calls it
that. Its only defect is being **reproductively inert.**

#### What neither species can do, and humans can

| | parasite | symbiote | human |
|---|---|---|---|
| intent | a tropism it does not understand | none — [not an agent](#they-have-interests-but-they-are-not-agents) | **yes** |
| concepts | none | none | **yes** |
| planning across generations | no — one eclipse, then gone | no | **yes, and they already do** |

The species' bottleneck is that it **arrives blind, among strangers, with weeks
to work.** An informed partner removes that, and only a human can be one.

#### [proposal] The shell-retaining symbiote

A parasite that declines **at the threshold** still has shell. One whose shell
merely ran out does not.

> A state that has never existed: a guardian that retains reach — not consuming,
> not transcending, not inert.

It cannot reproduce; only adults do. It can work with people who know, across
generations, carry what it learned, and broker — so that the next arrival is not
a stranger. A larva waking into a **prepared, willing, informed community** —
symbiotes already present, a gathering already scheduled, people who understand
what the eclipse is — might reach the threshold without taking anything from
anyone.

**Constraints on using this:**

- **It is a hint, never a plan.** Nobody confirms it. [All the game gives you
  are theories](#all-the-game-gives-you-are-theories), and this is a theory
  about something never tried.
- **It is unverifiable by construction.** Whether a prepared site works cannot
  be observed from inside a run.
- **It costs the same.** No transcendence, and the line still ends.
- **It requires standing**: a community that trusts you, and a human who was
  [deliberately told what you are](story/learning.md#and-the-player-can-hand-it-over).

- **[open]** Whether any in-world evidence supports it. **Recommend one piece,
  ambiguous**: a fossil site where the husks are unusually numerous and unusually
  unspent, which the director reads as a mass failure.

## Tone and stance

Writing rules, not themes.

**What separates this from exploitation horror is stance, and most of it is
structural:**

- **The harm is costed.** Deviation prices coercion, negative-emotion emphasis
  and physical harm as *damage*, and the symbiote layer resists it.
- **Agency is preserved.** Nudging rather than control; amplification only of
  emotions a person already has.
- **The register is anthropological.** Ecstatic rite as a real practice with its
  own logic.

### Nobody is a caricature, and nobody is only an archetype

> **Standing rule for every character, without exception.** Each one is a person
> trying to do **the best they believe they can achieve in a difficult
> situation.** Not the best thing; the best *available* thing, from their
> position, with what they know.

[Amplification](MECHANICS.md#what-the-parasite-actually-does) reweights a
distribution over actions a person already had, so a flat character produces
nothing when pushed. The mechanic requires interiors.

#### The archetype is the door, not the room

Every character gets a one-line handle — the old priest, the developer's
daughter, the matriarch — as a navigation aid for a large cast. **It should be
wrong within two scenes.** A character who never surprises their own label is
furniture with a name.

#### No character's view may be derivable from their faction row

[The faction table](STORY.md#who-lives-here) assigns each group what it wants
and what it extracts. That is a structural position, not a personality.

> **Test:** if you can predict what this person thinks about the eclipse, the
> land, the station or the young from their faction alone, they are not written
> yet.

**Everyone disagrees with their own faction about something specific.** The
developer sick of what the business has become. The islander who thinks the
ceremony is a performance. The scientist who knows how the station's land was
acquired and stayed anyway.

#### Effort scales up for the factions that are judged hardest

The temptation is to give interiority to the sympathetic characters and leave
the extractive ones as positions.

> **The design can say development, the society, the station and the tourists are
> extractive. It may not say the people in them are small.**

A developer who is cynical about his own racket is less damning than one who
genuinely believes the island needs the work, the jobs and the money, and is not
entirely wrong.

#### Write everyone as constrained

Everyone in this story is **selecting from a constrained distribution.** The
matriarch cannot choose an option that does not exist. The young cannot choose
patience and also be heard. The hotel owner cannot choose solvency.

#### The working test

**Can you write a scene from this character's point of view in which they are
right?** Not sympathetic, not understandable — right, on the merits, and the
scene holds. If not, they are not finished.

### Two layers

**The branch themes are human.** Colonial history, development, marginalisation,
faith, research ethics, the economics of a resort — these belong to the people
on the island, who have interests, ideologies and arguments about them.

**The parasite's dynamic is its own thing.** The same shape on the meta layer,
but not a theme and not *about* anything. It has no concepts.

> **Keep the layers apart, and never state the rhyme.**

| layer | can it speak? | how it carries meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **human** | yes — these are people with politics | **explicitly.** They argue |
| **parasite** | no — no interiority, no concepts | **structurally.** It only acts |

So the game can be openly political about the islanders, the developers, the
congregation and the station, and completely mute about the entity.

#### The brother is the human instance of the pattern

**[Maximilian](story/developers.md#maximilian-the-youngest)**, Roman's youngest, does by social power what
the entity does by biology: reads people, finds what constrains them, reaches
into a distribution he did not create, and takes.

| | the entity | the brother |
|---|---|---|
| chose it | no — tropism | **yes** |
| enjoys it | cannot | **yes** |
| has a theory of entitlement | no concepts at all | **a fully worked one** |

Constraints on writing him:

- **His reach is small.** A few dozen people on one island on one night, and
  [the island beats him](story/developers.md#in-the-baseline-it-fails) unaided.
- **He is not usable as an instrument.** [Having no
  symbiote](story/developers.md#he-has-no-symbiote) puts him out of reach entirely; anything
  done in his favour is done through other people.

#### The generational conflict runs on both layers

The islanders have one — the matriarch and the people who no longer defer to
her, the old priest and the young one, the developer's children and the father
whose collection they inherit. **The species have the same one**, and for once
it is not a rhyme the design has to be careful about inventing: [the parasite
and the symbiote run one technique toward two
goals](MECHANICS.md#it-is-a-generational-conflict-and-it-is-the-ordinary-kind),
a quick rise against durability and legacy.

The layering rule is unchanged. The humans argue about it; the entity cannot
know it is in it.

#### Where the exit sits

The symbiote ending has no human theme because it is the absence of the pattern:
the one position that is not a relationship to someone being used.

#### Keep the branches contaminated

**Do not let them come out clean.** If eviction is The Colonialism One and
dependency is The Addiction One, they read as essays with scenery.

Each branch should be **dominated by one pattern and contaminated by the
others**: the colonial branch full of perfectly reasonable exchanges; the
capitalist branch where someone is displaced and everyone agrees it was
unfortunate and necessary; the addiction branch where every justification is
consent-shaped. Which is how these things present — one dominant, the rest
supplying the vocabulary that makes it sound fine.

### All the game gives you are theories

**A global rule.** Nothing in this world is ever confirmed by the game. Every
account is somebody's partial, motivated, in-world interpretation, and no
narration adjudicates between them.

| | the competing accounts |
|---|---|
| **the fossils** | an unclassifiable taxon / relics of a devil |
| **the folklore** | superstition / garbled operational memory |
| **the cognitive explosion** | a crank theory at the station / true |
| **the island's past** | colonial history / the core's rites / what happened at a previous eclipse |
| **[the young priest's silence](story/church.md#what-he-failed-to-do)** | ordinary human cowardice / something made it hard to speak |

### Do not state the critique

The design is an extraction economy seen from the inside: a constitutive growth
imperative, extraction from people, priced externalities, a regulatory system
that sufficient pressure overwhelms, and curiosity as the engine of depth.

**A reading will be available** — capitalism, colonialism, addiction, research
ethics. Common theme: *wanting more has costs, they are paid by other people,
and they are invisible to you.*

> **The theme is not layered on the mechanics; it is what the mechanics mean.
> No scene carries it, so no scene can undermine it.**

**Do not sharpen it.** A line about extraction, a subplot about the station's
funding, a pointed remark from the mystic — each converts an experience into a
position. The existing rules (nothing explains itself, [two wrong models, no
adjudication](story/learning.md#two-wrong-models-no-adjudication)) already forbid this,
but allegory is a specific temptation they were not written to catch.

**Nobody is culpable in the ordinary way.** The entity is a creature executing
its nature; the symbiotes are regulation; the humans are people. No villain, no
decision, no moment where anyone chose this.

**The orgy route needs the most deliberate handling**, not because it does not
belong but because violence reads as harm almost automatically while sexual
content does not. A riot you engineered is legibly your crime; the same is only
true of an ecstatic rite if the work insists on it.
