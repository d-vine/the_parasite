# How the player learns anything

How anything in these documents reaches the player.

↩ [STORY](../STORY.md) · [timeline](timeline.md) · [DESIGN](../DESIGN.md) · [ECOSYSTEM](../ECOSYSTEM.md) · [MECHANICS](../MECHANICS.md)


The entity is ignorant and the interface is opaque, but the player needs
information — most urgently that a direct link is the difference
between symbiosis and extinction. The resolution is to route all of it through
**unreliable in-world interpreters.**

> **This file is one of two arcs.** What the humans work out runs alongside
> [what the parasite becomes](../STORY.md#two-discovery-stories-running-against-each-other),
> in the opposite direction and by opposite means: this one is told in scenes,
> that one is told by the interface. They meet only in the player.

### Humans are the exposition channel

Because the entity thinks with borrowed cognition, **inhabiting someone who is
investigating the anomaly means their reasoning becomes your reasoning.** They
theorise about the thing in the crater; you are thinking about yourself. The
entity acquires self-knowledge only by riding someone studying it.

> **Knowledge and threat are the same curve.** The humans closing in on what you
> are *is* your information channel. You cannot become informed without becoming
> hunted.

This gives the third act its shape: the scenes where you learn most are the
scenes where you are most nearly caught.

### The lifecycle must be assemblable, because the ending rests on it

[The final choice](../ECOSYSTEM.md#what-abandoning-transcendence-costs-the-species)
requires the player to understand what declining costs — under rules where
*nobody in the world is authoritative* and *the entity has no concepts.*

#### What actually has to be understood

Less than it first appears. **The reproductive biology can stay a gap.** What
the ending needs is only:

| | |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Parasites arrive at eclipses**, and have many times before |
| 2 | **One that succeeds leaves** — becomes something else, and is gone |
| 3 | **One that fails stays**, as a guardian spirit |
| 4 | **Guardians move through families and do not multiply** |
| 5 | ⇒ **every guardian here is a failure, and failures make nothing** |

Point 5 is an inference, not a statement, and **no character ever makes it.** It
is sufficient for the ending: *staying is ending.* The player does not need to
be told that adults merge timelines to reproduce — only that **nothing that
stays ever produces anything.**

#### Who holds which piece, and what each one gets wrong

| piece | held by | in the form of | wrong about |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. arrival at eclipses** | [the folklore](islanders.md#their-myths-are-the-most-accurate-account-on-the-island) | *a trickster who hides the sun* | **causation** — he does not hide it, he comes when it is hidden |
| | [the director's twenty years](station.md#the-director) | a correlation across a fossil locality and an eclipse track | nothing. He is simply alone with it |
| **2. success means departure** | the folklore | *and then we send him on his way* | **agency** — they believe the rite expels him |
| | [the core](church.md#the-core-is-not-a-corruption-of-the-society-it-is-its-original-purpose) | placation — *we keep the devil satisfied so he goes* | the same error, inherited through a different church |
| **3. failure means staying** | **nobody.** This is the gap | — | — |
| **4. guardians move through families** | the folklore | *a spirit that passes down a household* | **nothing at all.** It is exactly correct |
| | [the matriarch in communion](islanders.md#the-practice-is-technique-not-spirituality) | signature ages read off the network — **some very old, none new** | she reads it as continuity, not as a population that cannot grow |
| **the anomaly that joins 2 to 3** | [the young priest](church.md#the-young-priest) | a symbiote that is **new**, foreign, and resolves to nothing here | he has no idea he carries one |

> **Piece 3 is the keystone and nobody has it.** The folklore has the trickster
> and the guardians as **two unrelated beings**; the science has husks and a
> track; the network has ages. **Only something that can stand in several heads
> can put them together.**

#### Which is exactly what the player is

The one capability no human on this island has is **being more than one person.**

> The lifecycle is not learned, it is **assembled** — and it is assembled by
> possession. The player understands their own species because they have been a
> shaman, a scientist and a priest, and nobody else can be two of those.

That makes the cosmology a **reward for the core verb** rather than a lore
delivery, and keeps every individual account wrong.

#### Delivery rules

- **Every piece arrives as somebody's problem, never as information.** The
  matriarch's reading is a political instrument. The director's correlation is a
  career he cannot publish. The young priest's wrongness is a wound. The
  ceremony is a thing the community is arguing about. **Nobody is explaining
  anything to the player.**
- **It accumulates across runs**, like drivers — [entity knowledge survives the
  reset](../ARCHITECTURE.md#what-lives-where) because it was never in the snapshot.
  A first run cannot carry it; a late run should not have to re-earn it.
- **No scene states the conclusion.** Point 5 is assembled in the player's head
  or not at all. If a character says it, the structure collapses into exposition
  and [two wrong models](#two-wrong-models-no-adjudication) stops being true.
- **The entity never understands it either.** It has no concepts. The player
  understands; the thing they are playing does not — which is [the same
  asymmetry the ending runs
  on](../ECOSYSTEM.md#do-not-state-the-critique).

#### And the player can hand it over

[Brokerage](../MECHANICS.md#4-information-brokerage--between-humans) means the
assembled picture can be **delivered into a human** — almost certainly
[the finder](../STORY.md#the-opening), who is the only person holding enough of the pieces
to receive it.

> **Telling somebody what you are** — simultaneously the only route to a human
> who can act on it, and maximum exposure.

- **[open]** Whether a human who understands the lifecycle can act on it — help,
  refuse, or try to make the player stay. All three are good and they are
  different endings.

### What each discipline can discover

The exposition channels are not interchangeable. Each can reach a different part
of the truth, and **none of them can reach all of it.**

#### The biologist — who can detect *you*

- **The epidemiological signature.** Spread happens by skin contact along the
  intimacy graph, which produces a contact network shaped exactly like an
  infectious outbreak. Contact tracing is a century old. Someone doing field
  epidemiology in a town where people have suddenly started touching, fighting
  and falling into bed more than usual maps your movements precisely, with no
  idea what they are mapping.

  > **This is not lore. It is the player's own trail, and it scales with play.**
  > A careful run leaves a weak signal; an aggressive one leaves an outbreak
  > with a legible index case.

- **The endosymbiont.** The mitochondrial analogy is literal enough to be
  *found*. Mitochondria were visible under microscopes for decades before anyone
  understood them; endosymbiotic theory only arrived in 1967. So the symbiote is
  a structure present in nearly all human tissue — catalogued, assumed to be a
  staining artifact or a variant of something known. **Visible for a century, in
  the literature, unremarked.**

- **Which reveals the unclaimed.** Enough tissue surveys and some people turn
  out to *lack* it. Flagged as an anomaly in the data long before anyone can say
  what it means — and the player can learn who they are by riding the biologist
  who found them. **The lifeboat is identified by the science that is hunting
  you.**

#### The astronomer — who can detect your predecessors

Eclipse paths correlated against the localities of an unclassifiable fossil
taxon (see [The husk is in the fossil
record](../ECOSYSTEM.md#the-shells-are-in-the-fossil-record)). Deep history, not the present.

#### The counter-play: prevent a conversation

**Neither reaches the truth alone.** A fossil correlation is a curiosity; an
unknown organelle and a contact map are a curiosity. Together they are the
answer.

> So the third act hands the player a very specific objective: **keep those two
> people from comparing notes.** Not murder, not evasion — prevent a
> conversation. In a small town where everyone eats in the same room that is
> genuinely hard, and it means spending your remaining shell and credit on
> stopping two people from becoming friends.

### Two wrong models, no adjudication

| | gives you | wrong about |
|---|---|---|
| **the scientists** | a mechanistic account — testable, partial | everything the instruments cannot reach |
| **a mystic** | a metaphorical account — resonant, partial | everything the metaphor flattens |

**Neither is authoritative and the game never settles it.** Nothing ever
explains the cosmology; characters explain it incorrectly in two directions and
the player synthesises. That preserves ignorance as atmosphere while still
delivering what the player needs, and means the documents can hold facts (the
shell, the species, the three outcomes) that no line of dialogue is ever
responsible for stating correctly.

### The folklore is a real record

Every symbiote here is an expired parasite, so **parasites have come to this
place before — at previous eclipses.** The community's stories about possession
have a genuine referent, old and garbled, and they are attached to exactly the
event now approaching.

That is why the cults exist, why the folklore has observances, and why the
scientific interest in local historical anomalies is not idle.

The mystic may therefore be closer to right than the scientist on specific
points — not because mysticism works, but because the stories are old and were
once observations. That is a good reason for an island community to have
exactly the right superstitions, and it is free.

**[The islanders' tradition is the best-preserved copy](islanders.md#their-myths-are-the-most-accurate-account-on-the-island)**
— correct about symbiote inheritance, correct about what arrives at totality,
wrong only in attributing the eclipse to the visitor. And its observance is
[actionable in the strongest possible sense](islanders.md#the-ceremony-is-an-invitation):
it is the island's best transcendence event, and it was built to host one.

- **[open]** How much the *other* traditions retained. The cult is the same
  observation inherited through the church; the question is whether it kept a
  practice or only a secret.
